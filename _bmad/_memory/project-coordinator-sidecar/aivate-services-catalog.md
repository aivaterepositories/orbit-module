## Service Category 2: Custom CRM / Web Application Builds

### What It Is

Fully custom web applications built to manage a client's specific business operations. These aren't templates — they're built from scratch to match exactly how the client works.

### Who It's For

- Businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets or generic tools
- Companies with unique workflows that off-the-shelf software can't handle
- Operations-heavy businesses (property management, field services, logistics)

### Case Study: Temp Power Systems CRM

**Client:** Temp Power Systems (Chris Yates) — Temporary power solutions for construction & events
**What We Built:** Complete work order management system with crew scheduling, scope of value tracking, permit management, and automated task routing
**Tech Stack:** React, PostgreSQL, Salesforce API integration
**Result:** Replaced their spreadsheet-and-email chaos with a purpose-built system

### Pricing Framework

Custom CRM builds are scoped per project. Use this framework:

| Complexity | US Pricing | PH Pricing | Timeline | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $5,000–$10,000 | ₱120,000–₱250,000 | 3–4 weeks | Single-purpose app: tracker, dashboard, simple CRUD |
| Standard | $10,000–$25,000 | ₱250,000–₱500,000 | 4–8 weeks | Multi-feature app: multiple user roles, reporting, integrations |
| Enterprise | $25,000–$50,000+ | ₱500,000–₱1,000,000+ | 8–16 weeks | Complex system: RBAC, API integrations, automation engine, mobile-responsive |

**Scoping Process:**
1. Discovery call (you run this — see Phone Call Scripts)
2. You send a brief summary of what they need to Fiona
3. Fiona + dev team scopes the project and creates a proposal
4. You present the proposal and close the deal

### When to Pitch Custom CRM

- Prospect says "we're running everything off spreadsheets"
- They describe a process with 5+ steps that involves multiple people
- They mention they've tried off-the-shelf tools but "nothing fits how we work"
- Property management companies, field service businesses, logistics companies
- They need something with multiple user roles or complex permissions

## Service Category 4: n8n Workflow Automation

### What It Is

n8n is an open-source workflow automation tool (like Zapier, but self-hosted and infinitely more powerful). We build custom automated workflows that connect different tools and eliminate manual data entry.

### Service Offerings

| Service | US Pricing | PH Pricing | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple workflow (2–3 apps, linear flow) | $300–$750 | ₱8,000–₱20,000 | 1–3 days |
| Medium workflow (3–5 apps, conditional logic, error handling) | $750–$2,000 | ₱20,000–₱50,000 | 3–7 days |
| Complex workflow (5+ apps, API calls, data transformation, scheduling) | $2,000–$5,000 | ₱50,000–₱120,000 | 1–2 weeks |
| Automation audit + roadmap | $500–$1,000 | ₱15,000–₱25,000 | 3–5 days |
| Monthly automation retainer | $1,500–$3,000/mo | ₱35,000–₱70,000/mo | Ongoing |

### Examples of What We Automate

- Lead comes in via form → creates CRM record → assigns to team member → sends welcome email
- Invoice received → extracted via AI → logged in accounting software → approval notification sent
- Social media post scheduled → published across platforms → engagement tracked → report generated
- Customer support ticket → categorized by AI → routed to correct team → SLA timer started

### When to Pitch n8n

- Prospect describes any "we manually copy data from X to Y" scenario
- They mention Zapier but complain about limits or pricing
- They have multiple disconnected tools (CRM, email, accounting, project management)
- Operations-heavy businesses with repetitive data processes
- Any business spending hours on tasks that could be automated

### What It Is

This comes through Scale Virtually (Fiona's other company). Process engineering = documenting and optimizing a client's operations. VA staffing = sourcing and training virtual assistants to execute those processes.

### Why It Matters to You

Scale Virtually clients are a goldmine for Aivate upsells. A client who hires a VA to manage their CRM will inevitably need automation. A client whose processes are documented will discover gaps that technology can fill.

- Prospect mentions they have VAs but "they're spending too much time on repetitive stuff"

## Pricing Notes

### US vs. PH Pricing

- US pricing is in USD and reflects American market rates
- PH pricing is in Philippine Pesos and reflects local market rates (not just a conversion)
- PH pricing is generally 40-60% lower than US pricing in absolute terms
- Do NOT mix markets — a US prospect gets US pricing, a PH prospect gets PH pricing

### Discounts & Negotiation

- You have authority to offer up to **10% discount** on any deal without checking with Fiona
- For discounts above 10%, get Fiona's approval first
- Prefer offering **added scope** over discounts (e.g., "I'll throw in an extra workflow" instead of cutting price)
- Annual retainer commitments get an automatic **15% discount** on monthly rate

### Payment Terms

- **Project-based work:** 50% upfront, 50% on completion
- **Retainers:** Monthly, paid at start of each month
- **Large projects ($10k+):** Can be split into 3 payments (40/30/30)
- **Payment methods:** Wire transfer, PayPal, Wise
