## Compensation Structure

### The Basics

- **Base salary:** None. This is 100% commission from Day 1.
- **Commission rate:** 10-15% of closed deal revenue (see tiered structure below)
- **No cap on earnings.** The more you close, the more you make.
- **You are paid on revenue you bring in.** Every dollar the client pays, you get your percentage.

### Commission Tiers by Deal Size

| Deal Size (Total Revenue) | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to $5,000 | 10% |
| $5,001 - $15,000 | 12% |
| $15,001 - $30,000 | 13% |
| $30,001+ | 15% (negotiate with Fiona for specific large deals) |

Bigger deals earn a higher commission rate — this incentivizes you to pursue larger engagements and close enterprise-level work.

### Retainer / Recurring Revenue

| Period | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| First 3 months | Same rate as deal size tier above |
| Month 4+ | 8% ongoing (you continue earning as long as the client stays) |

## When Commission Is Paid

### Project-Based Work

- Commission is paid **when the client makes their first payment.**
- Standard payment terms: 50% upfront, 50% on completion.
- Your commission on the upfront payment is paid within 7 days of receiving it.
- Your commission on the completion payment is paid within 7 days of receiving it.

**Example:**
- You close a $5,000 custom CRM deal (10% commission = $500 total)
- Client pays $2,500 upfront → You receive $250 within 7 days
- Client pays $2,500 on completion → You receive $250 within 7 days

### Retainer / Monthly Services

- Commission is paid **monthly, within 7 days of receiving the client's payment.**
- First 3 months: 15% commission rate
- Month 4+: 10% commission rate (you continue earning as long as the client stays)

**Example:**
- You close a $2,000/month GHL retainer (deal value under $5k tier = 10%)
- Month 1-3: You earn $200/month (10% x $2,000)
- Month 4+: You earn $160/month (8% x $2,000)
- If the client stays 12 months: Total commission = $2,040

### Payment Method

- Payments via bank transfer, PayPal, or Wise (your choice)
- Commission statements sent monthly with breakdown of all deals

## Clawback Policy

### What It Is

If a client cancels or requests a full refund within 30 days of their first payment, the commission on that deal is clawed back (returned).

### How It Works

| Scenario | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Client cancels within 30 days, full refund issued | Full commission clawed back |
| Client cancels within 30 days, partial refund issued | Commission on the refunded portion is clawed back |
| Client cancels after 30 days | No clawback — your commission is safe |
| Client reduces scope (but doesn't cancel) | Commission adjusted to match new deal value |
| Client delays (but doesn't cancel) | No clawback — commission timing shifts with payment |

### Why This Exists

This protects both sides. You should be closing clients who genuinely need our services and will stick around — not pushing people into deals they'll regret. If you're qualifying properly, clawbacks should be extremely rare (< 5% of deals).

## Bonus Tiers (Performance Incentives)

These are optional bonuses that kick in when you exceed targets. They're in addition to your standard commission.

### Monthly Bonuses

| Achievement | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Close 3+ deals in a month | $250 bonus |
| Close $15,000+ in total revenue in a month | $500 bonus |
| Close $25,000+ in total revenue in a month | $1,000 bonus |
| Land a retainer client ($1,500+/month) | $300 bonus (one-time) |

### Quarterly Bonuses

| Achievement | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Close 10+ deals in a quarter | $750 bonus |
| Close $50,000+ in total revenue in a quarter | $1,500 bonus |
| Maintain 50%+ proposal-to-close rate for the quarter | $500 bonus |

### Referral Bonus

- If a closed client refers a new client who also closes: **5% bonus on the referred deal** (on top of your standard commission)

## What's NOT Included

- **Benefits:** No health insurance, PTO, or traditional employee benefits. This is a commission-based independent contractor role.
- **Expenses:** You cover your own LinkedIn (free or Sales Navigator), phone, and internet. If specific tools are needed, discuss with Fiona.
- **Equity:** No equity or ownership stake. This is a compensation-for-performance arrangement.

---

## Compensation Review

- Commission rates will be reviewed every 6 months
- If the business grows significantly, rates and bonuses may be adjusted (upward)
- Any changes to commission structure require 30 days written notice
- Existing deals in the pipeline at the time of a rate change honor the original rate
