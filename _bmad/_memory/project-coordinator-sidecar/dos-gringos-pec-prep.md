## Current Pain Points

1. **No dedicated lead manager** — responsibilities are split across the acquisitions team
2. **Acquisitions team is underperforming on lead management** — lack of focus and structure
3. **No systematized follow-up process** — leads are falling through the cracks
4. **Scripts not being followed consistently** by current team
5. **Leads not being nurtured** — resulting in lost opportunities
6. **Declining deal conversions** despite strong lead quality (recent performance drop)
7. **High cost structure** — paying acquisitions team 15% commission for lead management tasks
8. **No centralized pipeline ownership** — nobody owns the lead lifecycle
9. **Role confusion** — mixing lead management and closing responsibilities creates inefficiency

## The Role We're Building

### Lead Manager VA — Real Estate Acquisition Support

**Core Responsibilities:**

**1. Lead Intake Management**
- Receive inbound and outbound leads from multiple sources
- Monitor and respond to automated lead notifications (text alerts currently in place)

**2. Speed-to-Lead Follow-Up**
- Immediately contact new leads upon entry into the system
- Fast response time = primary KPI

**3. Lead Qualification**
- Conduct initial conversations with sellers
- Use Walker's existing "Triage Script"
- Identify motivation, timeline, and deal potential

**4. Appointment Setting / Handoff**
- Coordinate and pass qualified leads to acquisitions team
- Ensure proper endorsement of lead details for closing

**5. Follow-Up & Nurturing**
- Continue communication with leads not yet ready to convert
- Maintain engagement across the full pipeline

**6. CRM Management — GoHighLevel**
- Update lead status, notes, and communication history
- Maintain organized and accurate pipeline tracking

**7. Pipeline Coordination**
- Act as central point of contact for all incoming leads
- Ensure zero leads are missed or dropped
- Free up acquisitions team to focus purely on closing

## PEC Agenda — Talking Points

### 1. Introduction & Alignment (5 min)
- Welcome Walker (and Alex if joining)
- Confirm understanding of their business model
- Set expectations for the PEC: "We're going to map out exactly how your Lead Manager VA will operate day-to-day"

### 2. Current Workflow Deep Dive (15 min)
- Walk through their current lead flow end-to-end
- Where do leads come from? (Speed Dial, PPC, networking, auctions)
- What happens when a lead comes in right now?
- Who touches the lead and when?
- Where are leads falling off?
- What does the handoff to acquisitions look like today?

### 3. Process Design — Lead Manager VA Workflow (20 min)

**Build out these workflows together:**

**A. Lead Intake & Speed-to-Lead**
- Define response time target (e.g., under 5 minutes)
- First contact method: call, text, or both?
- What does the initial outreach look like?

**B. Qualification — Triage Script**
- Review existing Triage Script
- Define what makes a "qualified" lead
- What info must be captured before handoff?
- Disposition categories: Hot / Warm / Cold / Dead

**C. Handoff Protocol**
- How should the VA hand off to acquisitions?
- What fields/notes must be filled in GHL before handoff?
- Notification method to closer (GHL task? call? Slack?)

**D. Follow-Up & Nurture Cadence**
- How often should non-ready leads be contacted?
- What's the nurture timeline? (7 days? 30 days? 90 days?)
- Drip sequences vs. manual follow-up?

**E. CRM & Pipeline Management**
- GHL pipeline stages — define or refine
- Required fields per stage
- Daily/weekly pipeline hygiene

### 4. KPIs & Performance Metrics (5 min)
- Speed-to-lead response time
- Daily contact volume
- Qualification rate
- Appointment set rate
- Lead-to-close conversion (joint metric with closers)

### 5. Tools & Access (5 min)
- GHL access & permissions for VA
- Monday.com — will VA use this or just GHL?
- Speed Dial integration points
- Communication tools (phone system, texting platform)

### 6. Scaling Readiness (5 min)
- Current: 5–7 leads/day
- Planned: 1.5x–2x increase after systems are stable
- Does the process hold at higher volume?
- At what volume would a second VA be needed?

### 7. Next Steps & Timeline (5 min)
- SOP documentation timeline
- VA sourcing & MVA requirements
- Training plan
- Go-live target
- Check-in cadence post-launch

## Strategic Notes for Fiona

**Key wins to highlight:**
- Their marketing is already working (15x ROAS on cold calling) — the problem is operational, not lead-gen. We're fixing the revenue leak.
- Replacing 15% commission-based lead handling with a fixed-cost VA = immediate cost savings AND better performance.
- This is a high-ROI engagement — even a small improvement in follow-up consistency will translate directly to more closed deals.

**Watch for:**
- No SOPs exist — everything needs to be built from scratch. Budget time accordingly.
- Walker mentioned openness to VA bonus structure — keep this as a future conversation, don't lock in during PEC.
- Alex (partner) may have opinions — make sure both stakeholders are aligned.
- The Triage Script is their most valuable existing asset — build the entire qualification workflow around it.

**Process engineering approach:**
- Start with the lead intake → qualification → handoff workflow as the core process
- Layer in nurture cadence as Phase 2
- GHL pipeline optimization as ongoing
- Build SOPs as you go — document each workflow during the PEC
