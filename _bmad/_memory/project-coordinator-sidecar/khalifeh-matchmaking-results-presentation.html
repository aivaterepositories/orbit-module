BAE Systems — Direct Engagement

No prime contractor needed — we can work with BAE directly through their SBLO
▼
How BAE works: BAE Systems has a Small Business Liaison Officer (SBLO) program. We contact their SBLO, submit our capabilities, and get on their qualified vendor list. When they need MEP services, they can engage us directly — no middleman.
