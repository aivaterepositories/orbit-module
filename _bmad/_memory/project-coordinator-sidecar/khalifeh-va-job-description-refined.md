## Job Title

**Virtual Business Development & Executive Assistant**

---

## Company Overview

Khalifeh & Associates Inc. (KFA) is a Los Angeles-based MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) engineering consulting firm with 40 years of experience. KFA designs building systems for architectural firms, developers, and high-end residential clients across Southern California. The firm is known for quality engineering, LEED expertise, and long-standing relationships with top architectural firms.

---

## Role Summary

KFA is looking for an experienced Virtual Assistant to lead business development outreach, re-engage dormant client relationships, and provide executive support to the President, Jack Khalifeh. This role is **primarily BD-focused** (~60%) with administrative support (~30%) and light bookkeeping (~10%).

The ideal candidate has experience working with architectural firms, engineering consultancies, or the AEC (Architecture, Engineering, Construction) industry and understands how these relationships work. This is not a cold-calling sales role — it is a relationship-driven outreach role built on 40 years of existing reputation.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

### Business Development & Client Outreach (~60%)

- **Lead all outreach efforts** to re-engage dormant architectural firm and developer relationships using phone and email
- Make outreach calls via RingCentral on behalf of Jack Khalifeh
- Draft and send personalized re-engagement emails on Jack's behalf via Outlook
- Research prospects using KFA's 40-year Proposal Summary history before outreach
- Qualify prospects: identify active projects, gauge interest, surface pain points with current engineers
- Book meetings and lunches between Jack and prospective clients
- Manage and update the Client Outreach Tracker daily
- Provide weekly outreach summary reports to Jack
- Follow established outreach cadence (phone → email → monthly follow-up cycle)
- Capture feedback on lost proposals (why didn't we win?) to improve future bids
- Identify opportunities where KFA can take over from underperforming competitors

### Executive & Administrative Support (~30%)

- Manage Jack's calendar — schedule meetings, lunches, and client calls
- Draft and edit engineering correspondence, reports, and presentations
- Coordinate with vendors and service providers as needed
- Support the office manager with overflow administrative tasks
- Coordinate with IT consultants on equipment and software issues as needed
- Plan and coordinate client-facing events and networking logistics

### Light Bookkeeping (~10%)

- Support AR/AP processes in QuickBooks (graduated access — read-only initially)
- Track invoices and payment status
- Assist with basic financial data entry under supervision
- Full QuickBooks access granted after trust-building period (60-90 days)

## Required Qualifications

### Must-Have

- **2+ years experience in business development, client outreach, or account management** — preferably in the AEC industry (architecture, engineering, construction)
- **Familiarity with architectural firms or engineering consultancies** — understands the industry dynamics, how projects are awarded, and how architects select engineering partners
- **Strong phone communication skills** — comfortable making outreach calls, leaving voicemails, and having unscripted conversations
- **Professional email writing** — ability to draft warm, personalized emails on behalf of a company president
- **Adaptive and conversational** — not robotic or script-dependent. Must be able to read the room, pick up on client signals, and respond naturally
- **MS Office proficiency** — Word, Excel, Outlook
- **Self-starter** — can work with minimal supervision once trained on processes
- **Excellent organizational skills** — manages a tracker, follows up on time, doesn't let prospects slip through the cracks

### Nice-to-Have

- Experience with RingCentral or similar VoIP phone system
- QuickBooks experience (Online or Desktop)
- Knowledge of LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design)
- Experience with Constant Contact or similar email marketing platform
- Experience with CRM tools (note: KFA does not currently use a CRM — the Outreach Tracker is spreadsheet-based)
- ChatGPT familiarity for drafting correspondence and research

### Not Required (Changed from Original JD)

- ~~Bachelor's Degree~~ — Equivalent experience is acceptable. Jack confirmed this is not a hard requirement.
- ~~Salesforce experience~~ — Salesforce is being cancelled. Not needed.
- ~~Boomerang / Email Scheduler~~ — Not actively used.
- ~~In-person event attendance~~ — VA is remote. Jack handles in-person meetings and networking. VA handles coordination and follow-up.
- ~~Sales closing ability~~ — VA does not close deals. VA surfaces opportunities; Jack closes.

## What Success Looks Like

### First 30 Days
- Complete onboarding: Study Company & Conversation Guide, review Proposal Summary, learn KFA's history and differentiators
- Begin outreach to the first 20 priority prospects (provided by Jack)
- Log all activity in the Outreach Tracker
- Deliver first weekly outreach summary report

### First 60 Days
- Consistent weekly outreach cadence established
- Multiple meetings or lunches booked for Jack
- At least 1-2 project opportunities surfaced from re-engaged clients
- Feedback loop working: capturing "why we lost" data on past proposals
- Begin light admin support and QuickBooks read-only access

### First 90 Days
- Outreach process running independently with minimal oversight
- Demonstrable pipeline of re-engaged relationships
- Jack spending more time in meetings and less time worrying about marketing
- Expanded to second batch of prospects beyond the initial 20
- Full QuickBooks access (if trust milestones met)

## Screening Notes (For Scale Virtually Placement Team)

- **Priority skill:** BD/outreach experience in AEC or related professional services industry. This is the #1 filter.
- **Phone confidence is critical.** This role is phone-first, not email-first. Screen for comfort with unscripted phone conversations.
- **Adaptability over scripts.** Jack explicitly does not want someone "on a track." The VA must read situations and respond naturally.
- **Industry awareness matters.** The VA doesn't need to be an engineer, but they need to understand how architects select consultants and how project proposals work.
- **Rate reality at $15/hr:** We won't find a senior BDM at this rate. Look for someone with 2-3 years of outreach/account management experience who is eager to grow, ideally with AEC industry exposure even if not in a BDM title.
- **Bookkeeping is secondary.** Do NOT filter out strong BD candidates because they lack QuickBooks experience. QuickBooks can be trained; BD instincts cannot.
