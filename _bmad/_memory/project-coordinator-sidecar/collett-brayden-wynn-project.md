## Discovery Questions — By Process Area

### Session 1: Contact Research

**Understanding the Target:**
1. Who is your ideal client/prospect? Can you describe your ideal customer profile (ICP) — industry, company size, title of decision-maker, geography?
2. Are there any verticals or industries you do NOT want to target?
3. Do you have an existing list of prospects or past clients we can use as a reference for "good fit"?

**Research Process:**
4. Where do you currently find contacts? (LinkedIn, industry directories, Google, referrals, purchased lists, etc.)
5. What specific data points do you need for each contact? (Name, title, company, email, phone, LinkedIn URL, etc.)
6. How do you verify that an email address is valid before using it?
7. Is there a minimum quality threshold? (e.g., must have verified email, must be decision-maker)

**CRM & Data:**
8. What CRM are you using? (Walk me through it — show me where contacts live)
9. When a new contact is added, what fields need to be filled in?
10. Are there tags, lists, or segments the contact should be assigned to?
11. After logging a contact, what's the next step? Does it trigger an automated campaign, or does the VA manually move to outreach?
12. How many contacts per day/week do you expect the VA to research?

**Quality & Reporting:**
13. How would you like the VA to report on research output? (Daily count, spreadsheet, CRM report?)
14. Do you want to review/approve contacts before they enter a campaign, or can the VA add them directly?

---

### Session 2: Outreach

**Current State:**
1. Walk me through how you currently do outreach from start to finish — what does a typical sequence look like?
2. What channels are you using for outreach? (Email only? LinkedIn? Phone? Direct mail?)
3. Do you have existing proposal or pitch templates? Can you show me one?

**Proposal Process:**
4. What goes into a proposal? Is it a formal document, an email pitch, or a slide deck?
5. Is there a standard proposal template, or does it vary by prospect?
6. What information does the VA need to customize a proposal? (Prospect's industry, size, pain points?)
7. Who approves the proposal before it's sent — you, or can the VA send directly?

**Follow-Up Cadence:**
8. After a proposal is sent, what's your follow-up process? How many touchpoints and over what timeframe?
9. What channels do you follow up on? (Same as initial outreach, or different?)
10. At what point do you consider a prospect "cold" and stop following up?
11. What does a successful conversion look like? At what point does the VA hand the prospect to you for closing?

**Tracking & Reporting:**
12. How do you track where each prospect is in the pipeline? (CRM stages? Spreadsheet?)
13. What metrics matter to you? (# proposals sent, response rate, meetings booked?)
14. How often do you want outreach updates from the VA?

---

### Session 3: Email Management

**Current State:**
1. Walk me through your inbox right now — how is it currently organized? (Folders, labels, flags, categories?)
2. What email platform are you using? (Outlook, Gmail, etc.)
3. Roughly how many emails do you receive per day?
4. How much time do you currently spend on email daily?

**Triage & Categorization:**
5. If you had to sort your emails into buckets, what would those buckets be? (e.g., Client communication, Internal, Prospects, Vendors, FYI/Newsletters, Urgent)
6. What makes an email "urgent" vs. "can wait"?
7. Are there senders or domains that should always be flagged for your attention?
8. Are there email types the VA can archive/delete without consulting you? (Newsletters, notifications, spam?)

**Responding on Your Behalf:**
9. What types of emails are you comfortable having the VA respond to on your behalf? (e.g., scheduling requests, simple acknowledgments, info requests)
10. What types should NEVER be responded to without your input?
11. Can you show me 3-5 example emails and how you'd want them handled? (This will help us build response templates)
12. How do you want the VA to sign emails — as you, or as your assistant?

**Escalation:**
13. When the VA encounters an email they're unsure about, what's the escalation process? (Forward to you? Flag it? Message you on Slack/Teams?)
14. What's your expected response time when something is escalated?

**Access & Security:**
15. How will the VA access your email? (Delegate access, shared login via LastPass/Dashlane, separate account?)
16. Are there any email accounts or folders that should be off-limits?

---

### Session 4: Calendar Management

**Current State:**
1. What calendar platform are you using? (Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.)
2. Walk me through a typical week — what does your calendar usually look like?
3. Who currently manages your calendar? Just you, or does Hayes or anyone else schedule for you?

**Scheduling Rules:**
4. What are your available hours for meetings? Any days/times that are always blocked?
5. How much buffer time do you want between meetings?
6. Are there meeting types that get priority over others?
7. Do you have different availability for internal vs. external meetings?
8. What time zone(s) do you work across?

**Meeting Setup:**
9. What platforms do you use for virtual meetings? (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet?)
10. Does the VA need to create the meeting link, or do you use a scheduling tool (Calendly, etc.)?
11. What information should be included in a meeting invite? (Agenda, dial-in info, prep materials?)
12. Are there standard meeting templates for recurring meeting types?

**Coordination:**
13. If someone requests a meeting, can the VA accept/schedule directly, or do they need your approval first?
14. How should the VA handle scheduling conflicts or double-booking requests?
15. Do you want a daily briefing on your upcoming schedule?
