### Sector Priority for KA

**Priority 1: Platforms & Services — Maritime Solutions (San Diego)**

This is the strongest fit for two reasons:
- **Geography** — BAE Systems operates the San Diego Ship Repair yard. KA is in LA. This is the only BAE sector with a SoCal presence, making KA a realistic local MEP partner.
- **MEP Scope** — Ship repair yards, dry docks, and maritime maintenance facilities require constant MEP work: HVAC for enclosed industrial spaces, high-capacity electrical systems, plumbing and drainage for dock facilities, fire suppression, and utility infrastructure upgrades.

Contact: **sbadvocate.sdsr@baesystems.us** (San Diego Small Business Advocate)

**Priority 2: Space & Mission Systems**

Space facilities are MEP-intensive. Satellite assembly buildings need precision HVAC (temperature and humidity control for clean rooms), specialized electrical (grounding, shielding, redundant power), and plumbing (deionized water systems, cooling loops). Ground control stations and launch support facilities also require full MEP design.

Contact: **smallbiz.sms@baesystems.us**

**Priority 3: Electronic Systems**

Labs and manufacturing environments for defense electronics need environmental controls (HVAC for temperature-sensitive equipment), clean power distribution, and facility plumbing. FAST Labs (research division) may also have facility buildout needs.

Contact: **smallbusiness.es@baesystems.us** and **techscouting.es@baesystems.us**

**Priority 4: Intelligence & Security**

SCIFs and secure data centers need specialized HVAC (high-density cooling), redundant electrical power systems, and fire suppression. However, this sector's MEP work is often handled by SCIF-specialized contractors with specific security clearances, which may be a barrier for KA.

Contact: **smallbusiness.ushq@baesystems.us**

### How to Approach BAE Systems

Unlike AECOM (where KA would be a sub-consultant to a prime), BAE Systems allows **direct engagement** through their Small Business Liaison Officer (SBLO) program. This means KA can:

1. **Contact the SBLO directly** for the relevant sector
2. **Submit a capabilities statement** describing KA's MEP services, past projects, and qualifications
3. **Get on BAE's qualified vendor/supplier list** for MEP engineering services
4. **Be considered directly** when BAE needs MEP support on their facilities or projects — no prime contractor middleman

### Key Difference from AECOM

| | AECOM | BAE Systems |
|---|-------|-------------|
| **Relationship** | KA as sub-consultant to AECOM (prime) | KA as direct supplier/vendor to BAE |
| **How to engage** | Reach out to AECOM contacts, leverage past working relationship | Contact SBLO, submit capabilities, register on portal |
| **Middleman** | Yes — AECOM is the prime, KA supports under them | No — BAE engages small businesses directly |
| **Geographic advantage** | AECOM contacts are US West but projects are East Coast | BAE Maritime Solutions has a San Diego yard — close to KA in LA |
