**Khalifeh & Associates Inc. | Marketing Strategy Summary**

## The Opportunity

KA has 40 years of MEP engineering expertise and strong industry relationships — but no systematic marketing presence. This plan fixes that at minimal cost.

## The Strategy (3 Pillars)

### 1. LinkedIn Thought Leadership
- **2-3 posts/week** from Jack's personal profile
- Stories from 40 years of MEP engineering — authentic content that showcases KA's depth of experience
- 6 rotating content themes: career stories, project showcases, technical insights, partnerships, educational content, firm culture
- **Why it works:** Personal profiles get 5-8x more reach than company pages. Engineers trust content from other engineers 75% more.

### 2. Monthly Email Newsletter (Constant Contact)
- Monthly newsletter to architects, GCs, developers, prime contractors, and past clients
- Sections: Jack's commentary + project spotlight + technical tip + industry links + CTA
- ~7,000 contacts already imported and ready to receive content
- **Why it works:** B2B professional services email gets 36-42% open rates. 41% of small business owners say email is their most valuable channel.

### 3. Website & Digital Presence (WordPress + Google)
- Revamp website with dedicated service pages, CTAs, and modern design
- Optimize Google Business Profile (free — 70% more visits, 18x more search appearances)
- Collect Google Reviews from past clients
- Blog 1-2x/month (repurposed from LinkedIn content)
- **Why it works:** People searching "MEP engineering firm [city]" should find KA. Right now they may not.

## BD Integration

Every lunch meeting and networking event → LinkedIn connection → newsletter list → monthly nurture → quarterly re-engagement. **The flywheel compounds over time.** Already tracked in Trello.
