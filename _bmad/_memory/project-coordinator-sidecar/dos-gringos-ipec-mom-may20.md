## Key Focus Areas & Opportunities

**Lead Management:** The current outbound lead process lacks accountability, consistent follow-up, and script adherence—resulting in only 11 deals closed from 8 months of outbound leads. Two internal team members currently handle outbound follow-up with no structured process and will be replaced by the VA.

**Lead Quality:** The outbound cold calling agency (Speed Dial) was pulling low-equity lists, which lack the margin required for the fix-and-flip model. Walker and the team want high-equity properties, tired landlords, and sellers with room to negotiate. The ICP and lead qualification criteria are not yet defined and will be built during process engineering.

**Lead Volume Growth:** Dos Gringos has just partnered with a new marketing agency (Roar) and expects lead volume to double or triple from the current 5–7 leads per day. The process must be built to scale accordingly.

**Three Lead Funnels Identified:**

- **Outbound Cold Calling (Speed Dial):** High volume, low conversion currently.
- **PPC:** Low volume, high quality. Walker handles personally with near-immediate speed-to-lead.
- **Networking (Colton):** In-house real estate agent receives deals daily through reputation. Growing volume, getting overwhelmed.

Note: All three funnels will feed into a single Lead Manager VA who owns the pipeline end-to-end.

**CRM (GoHighLevel):** Two pipelines exist (PPC Leads, Outbound Leads) but are disorganized—leads in wrong pipelines, dead leads not cleaned, automated follow-ups inconsistent or missing entirely. Pipeline hygiene and management SOPs are needed.

**Process Documentation:** No existing documentation for speed-to-lead protocol, follow-up cadence, script adherence, lead qualification criteria, or handoff to acquisitions. All to be built during process engineering.
