## 2. SOP 1 — Lead Intake & Speed-to-Lead

This builds the first and most critical SOP: what happens from the moment a lead enters the system to the moment the VA makes first contact. From the IPEC, we know nobody owns this right now — automated text alerts exist, but follow-up is inconsistent.

**Questions:**

1. When a new lead comes in from Speed Dial, what exactly happens? (Notification type, where it appears, who sees it)
2. Same question for PPC leads — what's the notification flow?
3. Same for networking leads from Colton — how do those enter the system?
4. What's the current average time between a lead entering and someone contacting them?
5. What's your ideal speed-to-lead target? (We discussed under 5 minutes on the IPEC — is that still the target?)
6. For first contact — should the VA call, text, or both? In what order?
7. Do you have a preferred opening line or intro the VA should use for each lead source? (You shared the PPC opener on the IPEC: "Hey, this is [Name] with DGC Homebuyers..." — do we use that for all sources or different scripts per source?)
8. What information should the VA capture on that first contact? (Name, property address, motivation, timeline — what's the minimum?)
9. If the lead doesn't pick up on the first attempt, what should the VA do? (Voicemail? Text? Try again in 30 min?)
10. How many contact attempts before we move the lead to a different status?
11. What hours should the VA be making calls? Any blackout times?
12. Did you finish the call flow / triage script from your homework? Can we review it together?

## 3. SOP 2 — Lead Qualification

This builds the qualification process — how the VA determines whether a lead is Hot, Warm, Cold, or Dead. From the IPEC, Walker said the Triage Script exists but wasn't being followed, and the ICP wasn't formally defined yet. Walker's homework included defining qualification criteria.

**Questions:**

1. Did you get a chance to document the ICP / lead qualification criteria we talked about?
2. Let's define "qualified" concretely — what must be true for a lead to get handed off to acquisitions?
3. Walk me through your Triage Script — what questions does the VA ask, and in what order?
4. You said timeline is the #1 motivation indicator — anything beyond 60 days is generally not motivated. Is that still the rule? Any exceptions?
5. What are the other key motivation signals the VA should listen for? (Divorce/separation, inherited property, tax issues, tired landlord, financial distress?)
6. What property criteria matter? (Equity level — what's the minimum? Property type? Location boundaries? Condition?)
7. You mentioned you want high-equity properties with margin to negotiate — can you give me a number? (e.g., at least 40% equity? 50%?)
8. What are the immediate disqualifiers? (What makes a lead "Dead" on the spot?)
9. Let's confirm the disposition categories: Hot / Warm / Cold / Dead — what's the definition of each in your eyes?
   - **Hot** = ?
   - **Warm** = ?
   - **Cold** = ?
   - **Dead** = ?
10. When a lead is "Hot," what's the handoff process to acquisitions? (We'll build the full handoff SOP later, but what's the minimum for now?)
11. What notes or info must be in GHL before a lead can be handed off?
12. For "Warm" leads — what triggers a re-engagement? (Time-based follow-up? Life event? Market change?)
