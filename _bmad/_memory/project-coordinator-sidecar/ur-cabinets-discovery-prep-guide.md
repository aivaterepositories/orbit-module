## Pre-Call Questionnaire (Send to Client via Google Doc — Today or Tomorrow)

These questions should be sent to the client ahead of Friday's call so Marlon has answers in hand before the meeting.

### Business Goals & Pain Points

1. What's the main reason you're looking at a website revamp right now? What's not working?
2. What does a good month look like for you in terms of new projects/leads?
3. How many leads do you get per month right now, and where are they coming from? (Referrals, Google, social media, Houzz, Yelp?)
4. What's your average project value? (Kitchen remodel, bathroom, commercial?)
5. Are you trying to get more of a specific type of project? (e.g., more commercial, more high-end kitchens?)
6. Who are your main competitors in the Tampa Bay area?
7. Are there any specific areas or neighborhoods you want to target more?

### Current Website & Digital Presence

8. What do you like about your current website? What do you want to keep?
9. What do you dislike or what's frustrating about it?
10. Do you manage the Squarespace site yourself, or does someone else update it?
11. How often is the website updated — new projects, blog posts, etc.?
12. Do you know how much traffic your website gets monthly?
13. Are you happy with how the site looks on mobile/phones?
14. Is the "Get a Free Estimate" form generating leads? How many per week/month?
15. Is the Drift chat widget getting used? Is someone monitoring it?

### Google Ads & Paid Advertising

16. Have you ever run Google Ads before? If so, what happened — what worked, what didn't?
17. What's your monthly budget for advertising/marketing?
18. Are you currently spending on any paid advertising? (Facebook Ads, Yelp Ads, Houzz Pro, etc.)
19. Do you know what keywords or search terms your customers use to find you? (e.g., "custom cabinets Tampa," "kitchen remodel Plant City")
20. Are you open to running Google Ads alongside the website revamp, or do you want to launch the site first?

### Branding & Content

21. Do you have a brand guide — logo files, specific colors, fonts you use?
22. Do you have professional photos of your completed projects, or are most of them phone photos?
23. Are the before/after galleries on the current site up to date?
24. Do you want to keep the blog ("Junk Drawer News Feed")? Is anyone writing for it?
25. Do you have any video content — project walkthroughs, testimonials, process videos?

### Landing Page & Conversion

26. When someone clicks on an ad, what do you want them to do? (Call, fill out a form, book a showroom visit?)
27. Do you currently track where your leads come from — which ones turn into actual projects?
28. What's your typical sales process? (Lead comes in → showroom visit → quote → close?)
29. How fast does someone respond to form submissions or chat messages?
30. Would you want a separate landing page just for Google Ads traffic, or send people to the main site?

### Technical & Platform

31. Do you want to stay on Squarespace, or are you open to switching platforms? (WordPress, etc.)
32. Who has access to your Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and Google Business Profile?
33. Do you have access to your domain registrar? (Where urcabinets.com is registered)
34. Are there any integrations you need — CRM, scheduling tool, project management?

### Timeline & Budget

35. What's your ideal timeline for this? When do you want the new site live?
36. Do you have a budget range in mind for the website revamp?
37. Is the Google Ads budget separate from the website budget?
38. Who's the decision-maker — is it just you, or does someone else need to approve?
