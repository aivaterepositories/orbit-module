- [ ] Professional headshot (not a selfie)
- [ ] Custom banner (Aivate branded or value-prop focused)
- [ ] Headline = value proposition, NOT job title
- [ ] About section speaks to prospects, not recruiters
- [ ] Featured section has 2-3 relevant links/posts
- [ ] Experience section emphasizes outcomes

**Why this matters:** Your profile IS your sales page. 60%+ of connection acceptance depends on how your profile looks.

## Section 3: Prospect Research Process

Before you send a connection request, spend 60-90 seconds researching the prospect. This is what separates you from the hundreds of spammy InMails they get daily.

### What to Look For (60-Second Scan)

1. **Their headline and current role** — Are they the decision-maker?
2. **Company size** — Does it fit our ICP (5-50 employees)?
3. **Industry** — Is it in our target list?
4. **Recent posts or activity** — Anything you can reference in your message?
5. **Company website** — Does it look outdated? Do they have one?
6. **Tech signals** — Any mention of GHL, Shopify, Zapier, or other tools?

### Research Checklist

| Check | Where to Look | What You're Looking For |
|---|---|---|
| Decision-maker? | Their title/headline | Owner, CEO, COO, Founder, GM, VP Ops |
| Right company size? | Company page → employees | 5-50 employees (sweet spot: 10-30) |
| Right industry? | Company page, headline | Match against ICP Tier 1 or Tier 2 |
| Pain signals? | Their posts, company page, website | Complaints about manual work, hiring, scaling |
| Engagement hooks? | Their recent posts | Something you can genuinely reference |
| Website quality? | Google their company | Outdated, missing, or poorly built = opportunity |
| Tech stack clues? | Website footer, job postings | Mentions of tools they use |

### What Makes a Strong Prospect

Score each prospect mentally — you don't need to write this down for every person, but think through it:

- **Decision-maker?** +1
- **Right company size?** +1
- **Tier 1 industry?** +2 (Tier 2 = +1)
- **Pain signal visible?** +2
- **Active on LinkedIn?** +1
- **No website or bad website?** +1
- **Tech signal (GHL, Zapier, etc.)?** +2

**Score 5+:** Strong prospect. Personalize your message heavily.
**Score 3-4:** Decent prospect. Standard personalized message.
**Score 0-2:** Probably not worth your time. Skip unless you have a specific reason.

## Section 4: Lead Qualification Checklist

Use this during conversations (LinkedIn messages or calls) to determine if a prospect is worth pursuing.

### BANT Framework (Simplified)

| Criteria | What to Assess | Green Light | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | Can they afford our services? | They have revenue, mention investing in tech, or ask about pricing | "We have zero budget" / startup with no revenue |
| **Authority** | Are they the decision-maker? | Owner, CEO, COO, or "I make the tech decisions" | "I'd have to check with my boss's boss" |
| **Need** | Do they have a real problem we solve? | They describe a specific pain (manual processes, no CRM, etc.) | "We're just exploring" / no clear problem |
| **Timeline** | When do they need this done? | "We need this ASAP" / "This quarter" / specific date | "Maybe someday" / "No rush at all" |

**Minimum to qualify:** Must have at least Budget + Need. Authority and Timeline can be worked around.

## Section 5: Lead Scoring

Categorize every prospect into one of these buckets. Update as the relationship progresses.

### Hot (Priority 1 — Follow Up Same Day)

- Replied to your message with interest
- Asked about pricing or specific services
- Agreed to a discovery call
- Was referred by an existing client
- Described an urgent problem

**Action:** Respond within 2 hours. Get them on a call this week.

### Warm (Priority 2 — Follow Up Within 48 Hours)

- Accepted your connection request
- Replied but non-committal ("interesting, tell me more")
- Engaged with your content (liked/commented)
- Fits ICP perfectly but hasn't responded yet

**Action:** Send a follow-up message. Keep nurturing. Try to get them to a call within 2 weeks.

### Cold (Priority 3 — Nurture Over Time)

- Connection accepted but no reply to first message
- Read your message but didn't respond
- Doesn't fit ICP perfectly but is in the right ballpark

**Action:** Follow up per the cadence (Day 3, Day 7, Day 14). If still no response after 4 touches, move to Future.

### Future (Priority 4 — Check Back Quarterly)

- Explicitly said "not right now but maybe later"
- Business is too small currently but growing
- Not the right timing but good fit otherwise

**Action:** Add to quarterly check-in list. Engage with their content periodically. Reach back out in 3 months.

### Dead (Remove from Active Pipeline)

- Explicitly said "not interested, don't contact me again"
- Business closed or irrelevant
- Clearly does not fit ICP after research

**Action:** Remove from pipeline. Do not contact again.

| First messages sent | 125-200 |
| Conversations started | 40-60 |

| Proposals sent | 4-8 |
| Deals closed | 2-4 |

### LinkedIn Limits

Be aware of LinkedIn's activity limits to avoid account restrictions:

- **Connection requests:** Max ~100/week (free) or ~200/week (Sales Navigator)
- **Messages:** No hard limit, but sending 50+ identical messages/day will get flagged
- **Profile views:** No limit, but automated mass-viewing will trigger warnings
- **Key rule:** Personalize everything. LinkedIn's algorithm detects copy-paste spam.

## Section 7: Daily Routine

### Morning Block (30 min)

1. **Check messages** — Reply to any overnight responses (Hot leads first)
2. **Review pipeline** — Who needs a follow-up today?
3. **Send follow-ups** — Day 3, Day 7, Day 14 messages per your cadence

### Prospecting Block (60-90 min)

4. **Run LinkedIn searches** — Use the search strings from the ICP document
5. **Research prospects** — 60-second scan per prospect
6. **Send connection requests** — 20-30 per day with personalized notes
7. **Engage with content** — Like/comment on 10-15 prospect posts (genuine engagement, not "Great post!")

### Afternoon Block (30 min)

8. **Send first messages** — Message everyone who accepted your connection request today
9. **Follow up on warm conversations** — Push toward booking a call
10. **Update your tracker** — Log all activity, update lead scores

### End of Day (10 min)

11. **Quick pipeline review** — Any Hot leads that need attention tomorrow?
12. **Plan tomorrow** — Know which follow-ups are due and who you're targeting

## Section 8: Tracking Setup

### Option A: Google Sheets (Simple)

Create a spreadsheet with these columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Date Added | When you first connected |
| Name | Prospect's full name |
| Company | Their company name |
| Title | Their job title |
| Industry | Their industry |
| LinkedIn URL | Link to their profile |
| Email | If captured |
| Phone | If captured |
| Lead Score | Hot / Warm / Cold / Future |
| Stage | Pipeline stage (see Sales Process Pipeline doc) |
| Last Contact Date | When you last messaged them |
| Last Contact Type | Connection request / Message / Call / Email |
| Next Follow-Up Date | When to follow up next |
| Next Action | What to do next |
| Notes | Conversation details, pain points mentioned, objections |

### Option B: Notion Database

Same fields as above, but in a Notion database with views:
- **All Prospects** — Full list
- **Follow-Up Today** — Filtered by Next Follow-Up Date = Today
- **Hot Leads** — Filtered by Lead Score = Hot
- **Pipeline Board** — Kanban view by Stage

**Regardless of which tool you use:** Update it daily. A pipeline you don't update is a pipeline you can't manage.

## Section 9: Content Strategy (Optional but Recommended)

Posting on LinkedIn increases your visibility and warms up prospects before you message them. Aim for 2-3 posts per week.

### Post Types That Work

| Type | Example | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick tip** | "3 signs your CRM is holding you back" | Positions you as knowledgeable |
| **Client story** (anonymized) | "A property management company was running 200 properties off spreadsheets..." | Social proof |
| **Before/after** | "Before: 3 hours of manual data entry. After: automated in 5 minutes." | Visual impact |
| **Industry observation** | "Most [industry] companies don't realize how much money they lose to manual processes" | Engages target audience |
| **Question post** | "Business owners: what's the one task you wish you could automate?" | Drives engagement |

### Rules for Content

- Keep it casual and conversational (NOT corporate/marketing voice)
- Never pitch directly in posts — provide value, let people come to you
- Engage with comments on your posts (this boosts visibility)
- Comment on prospect posts before messaging them (warms the relationship)
