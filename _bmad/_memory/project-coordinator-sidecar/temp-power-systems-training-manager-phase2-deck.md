## Slide 3

# Work Order Calendar

Your scheduling command center.

- **Week and month views** — toggle between them
- **Color-coded by status** — see at a glance what's pending vs. active vs. complete
- **Drag and drop** — reschedule a work order by dragging it to a new date
- **Daily totals** — see how much work is scheduled per day
- **Monthly projection** — total expected billing at a glance

Use this view to spot gaps, overloaded days, and conflicts before they become field problems.

---

## Slide 4

# Spotting Conflicts on the Calendar

Things to watch for:

- **Overlapping crew assignments** — same crew booked on two jobs at the same time
- **Double-bookings** — too many work orders stacked on one day
- **Gaps** — days with no scheduled work (missed revenue)
- **Zero-dollar work orders** — pickups or already-billed items flagged differently

The calendar is your early warning system. If something looks off, fix it here before crews deploy.

## Slide 5

# Crew Assignment Builder

When editing a work order:

1. Open the **Crew Assignment** section
2. Add crew members by **Employee ID**
3. Set one person as **Crew Lead**
4. Save — assigned crew members will see this work order on their phones

**Multi-crew support:** You can create multiple crew groupings on a single work order if the job requires it.

Crews only see work orders with status **"Ready for Field Execution"** or later — not Pending.

## Slide 6

# Materials & Equipment

Each work order shows the materials and equipment needed.

- Review the materials list before crews deploy
- If something is missing or wrong — flag it and update before changing status to "Ready"
- The **Materials Report** gives a consolidated view across all jobs (useful for the shop)

Your job: Make sure what's listed matches what's available. Crews trust this list when they load the truck.

## Slide 7

# Crew Portal — What Your Crews See

When a crew member logs in on their phone, they see:

- **Today's assigned work orders** — tap to open
- **Job address** — where they're going
- **Scope of work** — what they're doing
- **Materials list** — what to load
- **Crew members** — who they're working with
- **Customer name** — who they're working for

They only see work orders assigned to them with status "Ready for Field Execution" or later. They never see Pending work orders.

---

## Slide 8

# Crew Portal — Clock In / Clock Out

The crew workflow is simple:

1. Open their assigned work order
2. Tap **"Clock In"** — records GPS location + start time
3. Do the work — mark tasks complete, upload photos
4. Tap **"Clock Out"** — records end time, shows total hours
5. Submit

**Why this matters to you:** Clock-in/out data feeds the dashboard metrics — hours worked, pay calculations, and job costing. Accurate timestamps = accurate billing.
