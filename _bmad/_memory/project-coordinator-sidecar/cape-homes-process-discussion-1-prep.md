## Email Management

**Current State**
1. What email platform are you using — Gmail, Outlook? How many inboxes will the EA monitor (personal, business, shared)?
2. Walk me through what happens when you open your inbox right now. What do you look at first? How do you decide what gets attention?
3. Roughly how many emails do you receive per day? What percentage actually need a response vs. informational/junk?

**Categories & Triage Rules**
4. If we were to sort your inbox into buckets, what categories make sense for your business? (e.g., deal-related, contractor/vendor, utilities, personal, TC-related, junk)
5. For each category — what's the expected action? Reply immediately, draft for your review, file it, forward to someone, delete?
6. Are there specific senders or domains that should ALWAYS be flagged as high priority? (e.g., title companies, attorneys, lenders, your TC)
7. Are there senders or types of emails the EA should NEVER respond to — only forward to you?

**Drafting & Tone**
8. Can you show me or describe 2-3 recent emails you sent? I want to capture your tone — formal, casual, somewhere in between?
9. Are there recurring emails you send over and over — the same type of message to vendors, contractors, or title companies? Those become templates.
10. When the EA drafts a response on your behalf, what sign-off do they use? Do they send as you, or as "EA on behalf of Scott"?

**Escalation & Boundaries**
11. What's the maximum response time before an email becomes urgent — same day? 2 hours? End of business?
12. If the EA isn't sure how to handle an email, what's the escalation process — flag it, send you a summary, text you?
13. Are there any email threads or topics the EA should never touch — legal, financial, personal?

**Cleanup & Maintenance**
14. Do you want the EA to clean up your current inbox (unsubscribe, archive old threads, organize labels/folders), or just manage going forward?
15. Do you use labels, folders, or stars currently? Any system we should keep or replace?

## Calendar Management

**Current State**
16. What calendar platform — Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple? Shared between you and Kristyn, or separate?
17. Walk me through how meetings get on your calendar today. Do people email you, call you, use a booking link, or just show up?
18. What does a typical week look like on your calendar — how many meetings, and what types?

**Scheduling Rules**
19. What are your available hours for meetings? Are there days or time blocks that are off-limits?
20. Do you want buffer time between meetings — 15 minutes, 30 minutes?
21. Are there recurring meetings already on the calendar (e.g., weekly TC check-in, team sync)?
22. Do you have different availability for different meeting types? (e.g., property tours only mornings, calls only afternoons)

**Approval & Autonomy**
23. Can the EA book meetings on your behalf without asking, or do they need to check with you first for every request?
24. Are there people who get automatic "yes" scheduling — like your TC, lender, or attorney — vs. people who need your approval?
25. Can the EA reschedule or cancel meetings on your behalf, or does that always go through you?

**Coordination**
26. Does Kristyn's calendar need to be managed too, or just yours? Do you have joint meetings that require both calendars?
27. Does the TC need visibility into your calendar, or do they schedule independently?
28. Do you use any booking/scheduling tools (Calendly, Acuity) currently, or should we set one up?

**Reminders & Prep**
29. Do you want the EA to send you reminders before meetings — how far in advance, and what info should be included?
30. Should the EA prep anything before your meetings — pull up the property file, confirm attendees, gather docs?

## Task Management

**Current State**
31. How do you track tasks today — app, spreadsheet, sticky notes, memory? Be honest — no judgment.
32. Walk me through what happens when a new task comes in — from a call, an email, a meeting. How does it get captured today?
33. What are the types of tasks you deal with most? (e.g., follow-up calls, document requests, vendor coordination, utility transfers, inspection scheduling)

**Daily Cadence**
34. Do you want a daily task review with the EA — a morning check-in where they walk you through what's open, what's due, what's blocked?
35. What time of day would that check-in happen, and how — call, message, email?
36. How do you want to be notified about tasks that are overdue or at risk?

**Priority System**
37. How do you decide what's urgent vs. important? Is it deal-stage-driven, deadline-driven, or person-driven?
38. If we set up a priority system (e.g., Critical / High / Medium / Low), what would "Critical" look like for your business? Give me an example.
39. When multiple tasks compete for attention on the same day, what's the tiebreaker — deal value, deadline, who's asking?

**Delegation & Follow-Up**
40. Can the EA assign tasks to your TC or vendors directly, or does everything route through you?
41. When the EA sends a follow-up to a vendor or contractor and gets no response, how many times should they follow up before escalating to you?
42. Are there recurring tasks that happen on a set schedule — weekly, monthly, per-deal? (e.g., utility check every Monday, insurance renewal tracking)

**Tool Decision**
43. Are you open to adopting a task management tool if you don't have one? Or do you want the EA to work within whatever system you already use?
44. If we set up a tool, do you want visibility into everything the EA is tracking, or just the highlights via the Stoplight Report?

## Closing Questions

45. Between email, calendar, and tasks — which one is the most broken right now? Where is the EA going to make the fastest difference?
46. Is there anything we haven't covered in these three areas that the EA needs to know about how you operate?
