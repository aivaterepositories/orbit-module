## Slide 3

# Your Role in the CRM

As a manager, you focus on:

- Monitoring jobs synced from Salesforce
- Scheduling work orders
- Managing the work order calendar
- Assigning crews to jobs

What you don't handle:
- Change order approvals (admin)
- Billing and invoicing (admin)
- User management (admin)

You see what's relevant to your daily work — nothing more, nothing less.

## Slide 5

# Jobs View — Your Starting Point

All jobs synced from Salesforce appear here automatically.

**Filters available:**
- Stage (Got Job, Get Ready, Executions, Maintenance, Removal)
- Owner
- Status

Click any job to open the full detail view.

Jobs move through stages as work progresses in the field — you'll see this pipeline update in real time.

## Slide 6

# Inside a Job Card

When you click into a job, you'll see:

- **SOV (Schedule of Values)** — read-only view of the financials
- **Tasks** — assigned to you or your crew
- **Work Orders** — all scheduled work under this job
- **Notes** — communication trail
- **Attachments** — documents, permits, contracts
- **Job Stage** — where the job is in the pipeline

You can review everything. Change order approvals and billing are admin-only.

## Slide 7

# Creating a Work Order

From any job detail page:

1. Click **"Create Work Order"**
2. Set the **scheduled start date** (required)
3. Set the **scheduled end date** (required)
4. Save — the system auto-assigns a WO number (e.g., WO-2026-001)

**Pro tip:** Create work orders 2–3 weeks ahead using **"Pending"** status. Crews won't see them until you change the status — this is how Wayne plans ahead.

---

## Slide 8

# Work Order Status Flow

**Pending** → **Ready for Field Execution** → **In Progress** → **Complete**

- **Pending** — On the calendar, but hidden from crew phones
- **Ready for Field Execution** — Now visible to assigned crews on their mobile app
- **In Progress** — Crew has clocked in and is working
- **Complete** — Work finished, ready for billing review

You control when crews see their assignments by changing the status from Pending to Ready for Field Execution.
