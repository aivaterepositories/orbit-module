# SLIDE 4 — The Problem

**What's Breaking Down**

- No dedicated lead manager — responsibilities split across acquisitions team
- No systematized follow-up process — leads fall through the cracks
- Scripts not being followed consistently
- Leads are not being nurtured — lost opportunities
- Declining deal conversions despite strong lead quality
- High cost — paying acquisitions team 15% commission for lead management work
- No centralized pipeline ownership
- Mixing lead management and closing roles creates confusion and inefficiency

**Bottom line:** The marketing works. The leads are there. The operational side is leaking revenue.

---

# SLIDE 5 — The Solution

**Lead Manager VA — Real Estate Acquisition Support**

One dedicated VA to own the entire lead lifecycle:

1. **Lead Intake** — Receive and monitor all incoming leads
2. **Speed-to-Lead** — Immediate first contact (primary KPI)
3. **Qualification** — Run the Triage Script, identify motivation and deal potential
4. **Appointment Setting** — Hand off qualified leads to acquisitions with full context
5. **Follow-Up & Nurture** — Stay on non-ready leads until they convert
6. **CRM Management** — Keep GoHighLevel clean and current
7. **Pipeline Coordination** — Central POC, zero leads dropped

**Result:** Acquisitions team focuses 100% on closing.

# SLIDE 8 — Proposed Lead Flow

**End-to-End Process**

New Lead Enters System (Speed Dial / PPC / Network / Auction)
  ↓
VA Receives Alert — Immediate First Contact (Speed-to-Lead)
  ↓
VA Runs Triage Script — Qualifies Lead
  ↓
**Decision Point:**
- HOT → Schedule appointment → Hand off to acquisitions with full notes
- WARM → Enter nurture cadence → Follow up on schedule
- COLD → Tag in GHL → Revisit at defined interval
- DEAD → Disposition in CRM → Archive
  ↓
Acquisitions Team Closes — VA updates GHL with outcome
