## Discovery Questions

### Eviction Process

1. What's the name of the third-party eviction company you use?
2. Walk me through the handoff — at what point do you decide "we're evicting"? Is there a specific number of days unpaid, or is it a judgment call?
3. Who makes the call to file — Willie, Haruna, or both?
4. What does the PM need to provide to the eviction company? (Lease copy, payment history, notice copies?)
5. Does the PM track the eviction progress, or does the third-party company handle all communication after handoff?
6. What happens to the unit during eviction — do you start marketing it, or wait until the tenant is out?

### Rooms-for-Rent (145 Alabama & 309 Holmes)

7. Walk me through how renting a room differs from renting a full property — is there a separate lease template for rooms?
8. How do you price individual rooms? Is it a flat rate per room or does it vary?
9. Do room tenants share common areas? Who's responsible for cleaning/maintaining those?
10. When one room tenant moves out, do you still pay all utilities for the whole house, or does anything change?
11. Are room tenants screened the same way as full-property tenants?
12. You mentioned these properties need specific maintenance — what does that look like? Is it because shared spaces get more wear and tear?
13. Are there house rules for shared living? Does the PM enforce those?
14. How do you handle conflicts between room tenants?

### New Property Acquisition Onboarding

15. When you acquire a new property and decide to keep it as a rental, what's the first thing that needs to happen?
16. Walk me through the onboarding steps — utilities, inspections, listings, anything else?
17. Who decides the rent amount for a new acquisition? Same market analysis process?
18. Does the PM coordinate any repairs or renovations before listing, or does that go through Ozbell?
19. Is there a checklist you follow today, even informally?

### Annual Wellness Check

20. How do you currently handle the annual inspection — do you already have a home inspector you use?
21. What does the inspector check? Is there a standard report format?
22. Who reviews the inspection report — Willie, Haruna, or both?
23. What happens after the report — does the PM create work orders for any issues found?
24. Is this for all properties including the rooms-for-rent, or just standard rentals?
25. When in the year do you do these — all at once, staggered, or on lease anniversary?
