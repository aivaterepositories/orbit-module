## Potential Responsibilities — Angela (Accounting & Billing)

| Area | What Fiona Can Take Off Your Plate |
|------|-----------------------------------|
| **Invoice follow-up** | Chase outstanding invoices, send reminders, escalate overdue accounts |
| **Billing reconciliation** | Cross-reference completed work orders against invoiced amounts — flag discrepancies |
| **SOV tracking** | Monitor Schedule of Values per job — ensure billed amounts align with completed phases |
| **Retention tracking** | Track retention balances and flag when retention release is due |
| **Payment status updates** | Keep the CRM billing status current — mark WOs as invoiced, reconciled, or pending |
| **Client communication** | Follow up with clients on payment timelines, PO numbers, or missing documentation |
| **Reporting** | Pull weekly/monthly billing summaries — what's been invoiced, what's outstanding, what's at risk |

## Potential Responsibilities — Sandra (Contracts & Compliance)

| Area | What Fiona Can Take Off Your Plate |
|------|-----------------------------------|
| **Permit tracking** | Monitor permit status, expiration dates, inspection dates — flag what's coming due |
| **Dig alert management** | Track dig alert numbers and expirations — ensure crews don't deploy with expired alerts |
| **Utility applications** | Follow up on utility application status, account info, and application IDs |
