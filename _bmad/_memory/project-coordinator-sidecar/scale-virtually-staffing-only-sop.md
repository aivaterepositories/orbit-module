## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the end-to-end workflow for handling **Staffing Only** engagements at Scale Virtually. Under this model, Scale Virtually sources and places a Remote Professional (VA), but **does not manage the ongoing service relationship**. The client assumes full management responsibility after placement.

This document ensures the PDEV, Placements, and Operations teams follow a consistent, efficient process from client assignment through placement handoff and post-placement support.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to:
- **PDEV Team** — Job description review, formatting, and placement request submission
- **Placements Team** — Candidate matching, client communication post-handoff, and endorsement
- **Operations Team** — Post-placement VA check-ins and retention support
- **Solutions Team** — Agreement and form maintenance

It covers all clients engaged under the **Staffing Only Services Agreement (MBA only)**.

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Staffing Only** | Engagement model where SV sources and places a VA; client manages post-hire. Also referred to as "MBA only." |
| **Remote Professional (VA)** | The virtual assistant or remote worker being placed with the client. |
| **Placement Fee** | Non-refundable fee paid by the client upon confirming a candidate and signing the Endorsement Form. |
| **Service Deposit** | Two-week deposit paid upon the VA's first day; protects against unpaid invoices or early termination. |
| **Endorsement Form** | Document signed by the client to confirm the hire, on or before the VA's first day. |
| **Candidate Request Form** | Internal form submitted by PDEV to Placements to initiate the matching process. |
| **JD** | Job Description — provided by the client or drafted by PDEV. |
| **IPEC** | Initial Post-Engineering Call — standard SV call; in Staffing Only, replaced by a JD Discussion Call (only if no JD exists). |
| **STP** | Standard Turnaround Processing within the Placements team. |

---

## 4. Process Overview

The Staffing Only workflow has **two paths** depending on whether the client has provided a job description.

### Path A — Client Provides a Job Description
> No client interaction required from PDEV.

### Path B — Client Does NOT Provide a Job Description
> One call with the client is required to draft and approve the JD.

---

## 5. Detailed Workflow

### 5.1 Assignment & Intake

| Step | Action | Owner | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|-------|
| 1 | PDEV Lead (Jessie) identifies a Staffing Only engagement and assigns it to a PDEV team member via round-robin. | PDEV Lead | Assignment includes client info, signed agreement, and any provided materials (JD, company profile, recording, etc.). |
| 2 | Assigned PDEV member reviews the assignment and confirms engagement type is **Staffing Only (MBA only)**. | PDEV Member | Verify: No SUF required. Check the signed Client Agreement to confirm Staffing Only terms. |
| 3 | Determine whether a Job Description has been provided by the client. | PDEV Member | This determines Path A or Path B. |

### 5.2 Path A — JD Provided by Client

| Step | Action | Owner | Turnaround |
|------|--------|-------|------------|
| A1 | Review the client-provided JD for completeness and accuracy. | PDEV Member | Same day |
| A2 | Transfer the JD into the **SV standard format**. Fill in any blanks based on role knowledge (e.g., recommended profile, qualifications typical for the role). | PDEV Member | Same day |
| A3 | Complete and submit the **Candidate Request Form** to Placements. | PDEV Member | Same day |
| A4 | Mark the task/project as completed in the tracking system. | PDEV Member | Same day |
| A5 | Placements takes over — contacts the client, proceeds with matching. | Placements | Per Placements STP |

**Key Point:** No client contact is required from PDEV when a JD already exists. Placements will be the first team to reach out to the client.

### 5.3 Path B — No JD Provided by Client

| Step | Action | Owner | Turnaround |
|------|--------|-------|------------|
| B1 | Gather requirements from the assignment materials (company profile, notes, recordings). | PDEV Member | Same day |
| B2 | Draft a proposed Job Description based on available information and role expertise. | PDEV Member | Same day |
| B3 | Schedule **one call** with the client — a JD Discussion Call. Invite Placements to attend. | PDEV Member | ASAP |
| B4 | Present the draft JD to the client on the call. Secure client approval for the JD during this single call. | PDEV Member | During call |
| B5 | Finalize the JD in SV standard format based on client feedback. | PDEV Member | Same day (post-call) |
| B6 | Complete and submit the **Candidate Request Form** to Placements. | PDEV Member | Same day (post-call) |
| B7 | Mark the task/project as completed in the tracking system. | PDEV Member | Same day (post-call) |
| B8 | Placements takes over from this point. | Placements | Per Placements STP |

**Key Point:** The JD Discussion Call is the **only** client interaction required from PDEV. The goal is to approve the JD in a single session. Placements is invited so they have context and can take over immediately.

---

## 6. Turnaround Time

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| PDEV processing (Path A — JD exists) | **Same day** as assignment |
| PDEV processing (Path B — No JD, post-call) | **Same day** after client call |
| Scheduling the JD Discussion Call (Path B) | **As soon as possible** after assignment |

**Rationale:** The sooner the Candidate Request Form exits the PDEV bucket, the sooner Placements can begin STP. Same-day turnaround is the standard expectation.

## 7. Upselling & Scope Expansion Guidelines

The primary objective of a Staffing Only engagement is **placement only**. PDEV should follow these guidelines:

| Guideline | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Default stance** | Reactive, not proactive. Do not push for additional services. |
| **When to bridge the conversation** | Only when, based on professional judgment and facts, the client's request reveals an obvious gap (e.g., a "unicorn" role that should be split into two realistic profiles). |
| **How to bridge** | Lay it out factually. Example: *"Based on our experience, a bookkeeper who also handles outbound sales is a rare profile. We'd recommend separating these into two roles. We also offer process documentation services if you don't have training materials yet — but that's optional and separate from the current agreement."* |
| **If client declines** | Drop it immediately. Focus exclusively on the Staffing Only placement. |
| **Current policy** | Reactive approach until we have more data on Staffing Only client profiles. Subject to change as patterns emerge. |

## 8. Agreement & Fee Structure

### 8.1 Staffing Only Services Agreement

- **One agreement = one placement.** Each VA placement requires its own separate agreement.
- Additional hires require: a new Staffing Only Agreement, a new Endorsement Form, and may incur an additional placement fee.

### 8.2 Placement Fee

- **Non-refundable**, charged when the client confirms the candidate and signs the Endorsement Form.
- Covers: sourcing, screening, interviews, and placement.
- Amount is specified in the individual client agreement (may vary; e.g., some clients may have $0 placement fee — refer to the signed agreement).

### 8.3 Hourly Rate

- Specified in the agreement based on the role and experience level.
- Tied to the scope of the role originally requested.
- If the client changes the role or requests a more senior candidate, the rate may change.
- The final rate is **always confirmed before the VA starts**.

### 8.4 Service Deposit

- **Two-week service deposit**, paid on the VA's first day.
- Protects against unpaid invoices and early termination costs.
- May be applied to final balances if needed.

### 8.5 Payment Methods

- Available payment methods are listed in the signed client agreement (Section: Placement Fee and Payment Terms).
- The placement fee and deposit amounts should also be reflected on the **Endorsement Form**, signed on or before the VA's first day.

## 9. Client Commitments & Protections

### 9.1 90-Day Minimum Commitment

- Clients commit to a **minimum 90-day engagement**.
- If the placement doesn't work out within that period:
  - Client must notify SV within **7 days** of identifying issues.
  - SV has up to **14 days** to present a replacement candidate.
  - A replacement search will be conducted at no additional placement fee.

### 9.2 Candidate Protection / Anti-Poaching

- All candidates introduced by SV are **protected for 12 months** from the date of introduction.
- If a client hires the candidate directly, contracts them outside the agreement, or hires them through another entity, a **$15,000 buyout fee** applies.

### 9.3 Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for:
- Providing an accurate job description (or cooperating with PDEV to develop one).
- Managing the Remote Professional day-to-day after placement.
- Paying the agreed hourly rate.
- Complying with independent contractor regulations.
- Ensuring compliance with contractor laws.

## 10. Post-Placement: Operations Support

Although the client is the primary manager in a Staffing Only engagement:

- **Operations will perform regular check-ins** with the placed VA to aid in **MVA retention**.
- This is an internal SV initiative — the client is not charged for these check-ins.
- Operations should monitor for early warning signs of disengagement or issues and escalate internally as needed.

## 13. Escalation & Edge Cases

| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Client's requirements suggest a "unicorn" role (unrealistic combination of skills) | Use professional judgment to recommend splitting into separate roles. If client declines, proceed with placement as requested. |
| Client asks about fees, deposits, or payment | Refer to the signed Staffing Only Services Agreement. Direct fee-related questions to the PDEV Lead or Solutions. |
| Client wants to add another VA | A new Staffing Only Agreement must be executed. Inform the client and loop in Solutions/Sales. |
| Placement doesn't work out within 90 days | Client notifies SV within 7 days. SV conducts replacement search (up to 14 days). |
| Client attempts to hire VA directly | $15,000 buyout fee applies. Escalate to Solutions/Legal. |
| PDEV discovers process gaps or training needs during JD call | Mention process engineering as an optional upgrade; do not push. If client declines, proceed with staffing only. |
| Unclear whether engagement is Staffing Only or full-service | Verify with the signed Client Agreement. Consult with PDEV Lead (Jessie) before proceeding. |
