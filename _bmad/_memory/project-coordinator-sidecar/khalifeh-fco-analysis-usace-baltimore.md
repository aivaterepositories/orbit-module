### Summary

- **Total MEP-relevant projects identified:** 33 out of 104

- **Key note:** AECOM, as a national firm, is likely the prime on several of these. Their West Coast team may manage or support contracts regardless of project location.

---

### Recommendation

Focus on Tier 1 and Tier 2 projects first when speaking with AECOM — these have the largest MEP scope and the highest likelihood of needing a dedicated MEP sub-consultant. The Tier 3 electrical projects are also worth flagging since they align directly with KA's capabilities.
