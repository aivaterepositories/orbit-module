## EA ROLE EXPECTATIONS (~10 min)

> "Great. Now let's talk about how this EA actually operates day-to-day — the expectations and the boundaries."

### Reporting Structure

> "First — who does the EA report to? Is it Scott, Kristyn, or both of you equally?"

> "If both — when the EA has a question and both of you are available, who should they go to first? We need a default so the EA doesn't get stuck."

### Autonomy Levels

> "I need to understand what the EA can do independently versus what needs your approval. Let me walk through the key areas:"

> "For email — can the EA draft and send responses on your behalf? Or do all drafts come to you for review first before anything goes out?"

> "For calendar — can the EA accept or decline meeting requests, or does every scheduling decision need your approval?"

> "For tasks — can the EA create follow-up tasks and assign them to vendors or your TC, or does that always flow through you?"

> "Is there a dollar amount where decisions get escalated? Like, anything involving a cost over a certain amount needs your sign-off?"

> "What should the EA NEVER do without checking with you first?"

*(Document every answer — these become the decision rules in every SOP)*

### Transaction Coordinator Relationship

> "Let's talk about the TC. Should the EA be coordinating with the TC directly — sending docs, requesting updates, confirming timelines? Or does everything go through you?"

> "Is there any overlap risk — things the TC handles that you also want the EA to handle? We want to make sure there's no duplication or confusion."

### Communication

> "How do you want to communicate with the EA day-to-day? Email, text, phone, a messaging app like Slack or WhatsApp?"

> "What hours do you need the EA working? And what timezone are you in?"
