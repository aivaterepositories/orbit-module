## Key Discoveries from the Call

### 1. Lead Sources — Three Funnels

| Source | Current State | Who Handles It | Volume |
|--------|--------------|----------------|--------|
| **Outbound Cold Calling** | Outsourced to Speed Dial. 8 months in, only 11 deals closed. Process is broken. | Two internal guys (to be replaced by VA) | 5–7 leads/day |
| **PPC** | Walker handles personally. Speed-to-lead is immediate (within minutes). High conversion. Two-step submission form. | Walker | Low volume, high quality |
| **Networking** | In-house real estate agent Colton receives deals daily from his reputation. Getting overwhelmed with inbound deal flow. | Colton | High volume (growing) |

**Lead volume is about to increase.** They just partnered with a new marketing agency called **Roar** (who also works with Scale Virtually). Expect leads to double or triple from current 5-7/day.

### 2. Current CRM State (GoHighLevel)

- Two pipelines: **PPC Leads** and **Outbound Leads**
- Automated text follow-ups exist but are inconsistent — some leads have zero follow-up
- Leads are in wrong pipelines (outbound leads mixed into PPC pipeline)
- No process for moving contact cards between pipelines correctly
- No accountability system for script adherence or follow-up cadence
- Long-term follow-up pipeline has leads with no activity at all
- Dead leads are not being cleaned out

### 3. Walker's Sales Philosophy (Critical for VA Training)

- **Personal touch over automation.** In a world of AI, human connection is what closes deals.
- **"The best houses come from distressed people, not distressed properties."** Focus on the person's motivation, not the property condition.
- **Timeline is the #1 motivation indicator.** Anything beyond 60 days = not motivated (with exceptions — some say 60-90 days because they need time to find a new place).
- **Open-ended questions first.** "Tell me, what do you have going on?" — let them talk.
- **Sensitive language.** Use "separating" not "divorce." Read the room based on motivation type.
- **PPC approach:** "Hey, this is Walker with DGC Homebuyers. We're one of Kansas City's biggest homebuyers. We saw you filled out our form online. You asked about a cash offer. Tell me, what do you have going on?"
- **Outbound approach:** Different posture — they didn't contact us, but they consented. Ask clarifying questions about what they're trying to accomplish.
- **Follow-up intensity wins deals.** Walker shared a live example: lead from Thursday, under contract by Tuesday because of persistent, caring follow-up.

### 4. Lead Qualification Criteria — NOT DEFINED YET

- No concrete ICP or lead qualification checklist exists
- Working with marketing agency (Roar) on defining this
- Key issue discovered: cold call agency (Speed Dial) was pulling **low-equity lists** instead of **high-equity lists** — wrong leads entirely
- What they want: **High equity, tired landlords, people with margin for negotiation**
- What they don't want: Low equity, creative finance, sub-two deals
- Walker to document ICP as part of his homework

### 5. VA Role — Evolution Path

**Phase 1 (Immediate):** Lead Management
- Own the lead pipeline across all three sources
- Speed-to-lead follow-up
- Run triage/qualification script
- CRM management and pipeline hygiene
- Follow-up and nurturing cadence
- Hand off qualified leads to acquisitions

**Phase 2 (After VA is comfortable, ~3-6 months):** Direct Closing
- Walker's ideal: VA learns the acquisition process deeply enough to close deals over the phone without an acquisitions person
- This saves commission costs (currently paying acquisitions team for closing)
- Walker said: "I don't expect you to find somebody like that day one, but it would be nice to have somebody with the skillset to learn something like that over time."

**Phase 3 (Supplemental):** Underwriting Support with Colton
- Colton (in-house real estate agent) gets overwhelmed with deal flow
- VA could sort and pre-screen networking leads for Colton
- VA would do preliminary underwriting — determine if an opportunity is worth pursuing based on numbers
- VA needs to understand comps at a basic level (what they are, what they mean — not necessarily how to pull them)
- Colton focuses purely on negotiating while VA handles the funnel feeding him
- **Three funnels → One VA → Colton coordination model:**
  - Outbound leads → VA qualifies → hand off or close
  - PPC leads → VA qualifies → hand off or close
  - Networking leads → VA pre-screens → passes viable deals to Colton

### 6. Full-Time VA Confirmed

- Full-time, 8 hours/day, 40 hours/week, CST
- Lead management may not fill all 8 hours initially — remaining bandwidth goes to underwriting support / Colton coordination
- Walker: "That gives me more space or more capacity to look for other ways that we can utilize this VA."

### 7. Automation Philosophy

- **Automate:** Tasks, reminders, triggers, notifications (e.g., "follow up with this lead" reminder)
- **Do NOT automate:** Communication. Calls, texts, and emails should be personal, from a real person.
- **Exception:** Simple template messages can be automated — welcome messages, confirmation messages, "your home is sold" type comms
- Walker: "I firmly believe that a lot of the reason I get deals is because I care more than the other people."

### 8. Bonus Structure

- Walker is open to bonuses, especially once the VA is involved in sales/closing
- No specifics defined yet — to be discussed in a future session
- Walker: "We are more than willing to pay people bonuses. We do it at our construction side."
- Fiona to provide examples of how other clients structure VA bonuses

### 9. The Two Outbound Guys

- Currently two people handle outbound lead follow-up
- They "just do it" — no process, no accountability, no tracking
- They will be **replaced** by the Lead Manager VA
- This is a cost-saving move (commission-based → fixed-cost VA)
