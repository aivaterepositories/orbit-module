## How to Use This Guide

This document covers the most common objections you'll hear when cold calling businesses about office leasing opportunities. For each objection, you'll find:

1. **What they're really saying** — the underlying concern
2. **Recommended response** — what to say
3. **Goal** — what outcome you're aiming for

**Golden rules:**
- Never argue or get defensive
- Acknowledge their concern first — always
- Your job is to keep the door open, not force it open
- When in doubt, route to Nick

---

## Objection 1: "We're not interested."

**What they're really saying:** "I don't know enough to be interested" or "I'm busy and this feels like a sales call."

**Response:**
> "I completely understand — I appreciate your time. Just so you know, this isn't a hard sell at all. We have move-in-ready professional office suites available in a prime Roswell location, and our management team asked me to reach out to a select group of businesses. If anything changes down the road, would it be okay if I sent you a quick email with the details? That way you have it on file if your needs shift."

**Goal:** Capture email for nurture list. Plant the seed.

---

## Objection 2: "We already have office space." / "We're happy where we are."

**What they're really saying:** "We have a current solution and see no reason to change."

**Response:**
> "That's great to hear — glad you're settled. Out of curiosity, when does your current lease come up for renewal? A lot of businesses like to start exploring options 6 to 12 months out so they're not making a rushed decision. I'd love to at least send you some info so it's there when the time comes."

**Goal:** Capture lease expiration date. Set long-term follow-up reminder.

**If they share their lease expiry:**
> "Perfect — I'll make a note of that. Would it be okay if I reach back out about [3–6 months before expiry] so you have time to compare options?"

---

## Objection 3: "We just signed a new lease."

**What they're really saying:** "Your timing is off."

**Response:**
> "Congratulations on the new space! May I ask how long the term is? I like to keep track so that when the time comes, you're aware of what's available in the area. The Roswell market is growing fast and options move quickly."

**Goal:** Capture lease term/expiry. Add to long-term nurture. This is a future opportunity, not a dead end.

---

## Objection 4: "How much is it?" / "What's the rent?"

**What they're really saying:** "I might be interested, but price is a factor."

**Response:**
> "Great question — that tells me you're at least thinking about it! The spaces are competitively priced and offered as a full-service lease, which means your base rent covers utilities, janitorial, 24/7 security, and building access. For the specifics, Nick Kavali, our Property Manager, would be the best person to walk you through the numbers based on your particular needs. Would you like me to set up a quick call with him, or I can send you an email with the property overview first?"

**Goal:** Route to Nick for pricing. NEVER quote $/RSF yourself.

---

## Objection 5: "What does 'full-service lease' mean?"

**What they're really saying:** "I need to understand the value before I consider it."

**Response:**
> "Great question. A full-service lease means that your base rent payment includes utilities, nighttime janitorial services, daytime Day Porter services, 24/7 building surveillance, and 24/7 access controls for your staff. So it simplifies your overhead significantly — one payment covers the big operational items."

**Goal:** Educate and build value. This is a strong selling point — lean into it.

---

## Objection 6: "Send me an email." (Brush-off)

**What they're really saying:** Could be genuine interest OR a polite way to end the call.

**Response (if it feels genuine):**
> "Absolutely — I'd be happy to. What's the best email address? And just so I can include the right details, are you looking at something in the 2,800 to 8,000 square foot range, or would a larger combined space be more suitable?"

**Response (if it feels like a brush-off):**
> "Of course! I'll send it right over. Just to make sure I'm sending the right information — would this be for your current team size, or are you planning to grow? That way I can include the most relevant suite."

**Goal:** Qualify even during the brush-off. Capture email AND at least one data point (size need, team size, growth plans).

---

## Objection 7: "I need to talk to my partner / business partner / the team first."

**What they're really saying:** "I'm not the sole decision maker" or "I need buy-in."

**Response:**
> "That makes total sense — these decisions are usually a team effort. I'll send you an email with the full property details so you have something to share with them. Would it be helpful if I also included some photos of the space? And is there a good day next week for me to follow up after you've had a chance to discuss?"

**Goal:** Arm them with materials to share. Set a specific follow-up date.

---

## Objection 8: "We work remotely / we don't need office space."

**What they're really saying:** "Physical office isn't a priority for us."

**Response:**
> "I hear that a lot these days — and it totally makes sense for some businesses. Just in case things evolve — a lot of companies are finding that having a professional meeting space or a hybrid setup helps with client-facing interactions and team culture. If that ever becomes relevant for you, I'd love for you to know about this option. Can I send you a quick email for your files?"

**Goal:** Respect their decision. Light touch — plant the seed for hybrid/future needs.

---

## Objection 9: "Roswell is too far." / "That location doesn't work for us."

**What they're really saying:** "Geography is a dealbreaker."

**Response:**
> "I completely understand — location is everything. Just in case it helps, the property is less than two miles from GA-400 off the Mansell Road exit, so it's very accessible from most of North Metro Atlanta. But if Roswell isn't the right fit for you, I appreciate your time. Would you happen to know anyone in your network who might be looking for office space in this area?"

**Goal:** Confirm it's truly a location issue. Ask for referrals.

---

## Objection 10: "We're downsizing / cutting costs."

**What they're really saying:** "Budget is tight and we're not expanding."

**Response:**
> "I understand — a lot of businesses are being strategic about overhead right now. Actually, that might be worth a quick look. We have a smaller suite available — about 2,800 square feet — that's a full-service lease, so your rent covers utilities, janitorial, security, and access. It could actually simplify and potentially reduce your total office overhead compared to managing those services separately. Would it be worth a quick email with the details?"

**Goal:** Reframe the full-service lease as a cost-saving advantage. Position Suite 240 as the right-size option.

---

## Objection 11: "I'm too busy right now."

**What they're really saying:** "Not a good time to talk."

**Response:**
> "Absolutely — I respect your time. I'll be quick: we have professional office space available in Roswell and I wanted to make sure you knew about it. Can I send you a brief email with the details? That way you can look at it whenever it's convenient. What's the best email?"

**Goal:** Keep it to 15 seconds. Get the email. Follow up later.

---

## Objection 12: "I've never heard of WePartner Commercial."

**What they're really saying:** "Who are you and why should I trust this?"

**Response:**
> "That's fair — let me give you a quick background. WePartner Commercial manages Mansell Office Park in Roswell. It's a well-established two-story brick office building at the corner of Mansell and Houze Roads. We've got a great mix of tenants — Keller Williams, a coworking space, medical, insurance, and professional services firms. Nick Kavali is the Property Manager and he'd love to introduce you to the property if you're interested."

**Goal:** Build credibility through tenant mix and property details. Route to Nick for relationship building.

---

## Escalation Rules

**Transfer to Nick immediately if:**
- Prospect is actively looking for space RIGHT NOW
- Prospect wants specific lease terms, pricing, or negotiations
- Prospect asks technical questions about the building you can't answer
- Prospect is upset or confrontational
- Prospect wants to schedule a tour

**Do NOT try to handle on your own:**
- Any negotiation on price or terms
- Promises about availability, move-in dates, or build-out
- Anything that could be construed as a binding commitment

**When in doubt:** "That's a great question — let me get you connected with Nick who can give you the most accurate answer on that."
