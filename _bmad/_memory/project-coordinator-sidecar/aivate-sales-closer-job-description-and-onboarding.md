## PART 1: JOB DESCRIPTION

### Title: Sales Closer — Aivate

### About Aivate

Aivate is a technology services company that builds automation systems, custom CRMs, websites, and workflow solutions for small-to-medium businesses. We operate across the US and Philippines markets. Our clients include property management companies, field service businesses, real estate brokerages, digital agencies, coaches, and e-commerce brands.

We're a small, fast team. No bureaucracy, no corporate nonsense. You'll have full autonomy to source leads, run calls, and close deals.

### The Role

You are the outbound sales engine. Your job is to find prospects on LinkedIn, build relationships through messaging, get them on discovery calls, and close deals. You own the entire pipeline from first connection request to signed contract.

This is a **commission-only** role. No base salary. You eat what you kill — and we've built the entire system to help you kill a lot.

### What You'll Do

- **Prospect:** Source leads on LinkedIn using our ICP and search filters
- **Connect:** Send personalized connection requests and follow-up messages
- **Qualify:** Determine if a prospect has budget, authority, need, and timeline
- **Discover:** Run discovery calls to understand prospect pain points and map them to services
- **Close:** Present solutions, handle objections, and close deals
- **Track:** Log all activity in the tracking system (daily)
- **Report:** Submit weekly activity and pipeline reports

### What You Won't Do

- You won't build or deliver the actual services (that's the dev team)
- You won't create proposals from scratch (templates provided, Fiona scopes complex deals)
- You won't manage client relationships post-sale (handoff to delivery team)

### Requirements

- Comfortable with outbound sales (LinkedIn-first approach)
- Can run a discovery call confidently and casually (not robotic or salesy)
- Self-motivated — nobody will micromanage you
- Organized enough to track pipeline and follow up consistently
- Understands (or can quickly learn) what CRMs, automations, and workflows are
- Available for calls during US business hours (for US market) or PH business hours (for PH market)

### Compensation

- **Structure:** 100% commission from Day 1 (see Compensation Plan for full details)
- **Commission:** 15-20% of closed deal revenue
- **Earning potential:** Directly tied to your activity level and close rate
- **No cap on earnings**

### Who You'll Work With

| Person | Role | When to Contact |
|---|---|---|
| **Fiona** | CEO / Founder | Complex deal scoping, pricing exceptions, strategic decisions |
| **Marky** | Tech Lead / Automation | Technical questions about GHL, n8n, feasibility |
| **Cob** | Developer | Technical questions about web builds, CRM feasibility |

## PART 3: LINKEDIN PROFILE OPTIMIZATION

Your LinkedIn profile is your storefront. Every prospect you message will check your profile before deciding whether to reply. Make it count.

### Profile Photo

- Professional headshot (not a selfie, not a group photo)
- Good lighting, clean background
- Friendly expression — you're approachable, not corporate
- Business casual (no suit needed, but not a t-shirt)

### Banner Image

- Use a banner that communicates what Aivate does
- Options: Aivate branded banner, or a clean graphic with tagline like "Helping businesses automate, scale, and grow"
- Ask Fiona/Cob for a branded banner if needed

### Headline

Don't use your job title. Use a value proposition.

**Bad:** "Sales Representative at Aivate"

**Good options:**
- "Helping businesses automate their operations and scale faster | Aivate"
- "I help service businesses eliminate manual work with smart automation | Aivate"
- "Business automation for growing companies — CRM, workflows, and more | Aivate"

### About Section

Write this like you're talking to a prospect, not a recruiter. Keep it casual and value-focused.

**Template:**

> Most small businesses are stuck doing things manually — following up with leads by hand, copying data between tools, managing operations off spreadsheets.
>
> I work with business owners who are ready to fix that.
>
> At Aivate, we build the systems that let you focus on growing your business instead of running it:
>
> - CRM and lead management automation (GoHighLevel)
> - Custom web applications and dashboards
> - Workflow automation (n8n — like Zapier, but way more powerful)
> - E-commerce stores (Shopify)
> - Landing pages and websites
>
> If you're spending hours on tasks that a system could handle in seconds, let's talk.
>
> Message me or book a quick call — no pressure, no pitch decks, just a conversation about what's possible.

### Featured Section

Pin 2-3 items:
- Link to the Aivate Catalogue website (when live)
- A post about a client success story
- A post about automation or efficiency (to signal expertise)

### Experience

- Title: "Business Development" or "Growth Partner" at Aivate
- Description: Focus on outcomes you help clients achieve, not internal tasks

## PART 7: FAQ FOR THE CLOSER

### "What if a prospect asks a technical question I can't answer?"

Say: "That's a great question — I want to make sure I give you the right answer. Let me check with our technical team and get back to you today." Then Slack/message Marky or Cob. Get the answer. Follow up within a few hours.

### "What if a prospect wants a discount bigger than 10%?"

You can offer up to 10% on your own. Above that, message Fiona with the deal details and what discount they're asking for. She'll respond quickly.

### "What if a prospect needs something we don't offer?"

Be honest: "That's not something we specialize in, but I can point you in the right direction." Don't try to sell something we can't deliver. If you're unsure whether we can do it, ask Fiona.

### "How fast should I respond to messages?"

Within 2 hours during business hours. Speed matters — the faster you respond, the more likely they are to stay engaged.

### "What if I get a referral from a current client?"

Referrals are gold. Treat them as Hot leads. Mention the referrer by name in your first message. These have the highest close rate.

### "What tools do I need?"

- LinkedIn (free or Sales Navigator — check with Fiona on budget)
- Google Sheets or the tracking tool Fiona provides
- Zoom or Google Meet for discovery calls
- A phone for calls (if applicable)
- Slack/Discord for team communication

### "What if I'm having a slow week?"

It happens. Review your activity numbers — are you sending enough connection requests? Are your messages personalized or generic? Are you following up consistently? Volume fixes most slow weeks. If it persists, schedule a call with Fiona to troubleshoot.

### "Can I sell to my own network?"

Yes. If you have existing contacts who fit the ICP, absolutely reach out. Same commission applies.

### "Who handles contracts and invoicing?"

Fiona handles contracts and invoicing after you close the deal. You hand off the client details, Fiona sends the contract and invoice.
