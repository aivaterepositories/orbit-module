## Overview

This document defines the exact follow-up rhythm for every contact in Jack's re-engagement campaign. The cadence is designed to be **professional and persistent without being aggressive**. These are long-term relationships (10-30+ years) — the tone must always be respectful, warm, and never desperate.

**Key principle:** This is NOT cold outreach. These people know and respect Jack. The follow-up should feel like a professional courtesy, not a sales push. If someone doesn't respond after the full cadence, they're busy — not uninterested. We park them and try again in a few months.

---

## Contact Categories

| Category | Definition | Follow-Up Priority |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **HOT** | Wants to meet, asked Jack to call, enthusiastic about reconnecting | **IMMEDIATE** — Notify Jack within 1 hour. Coordinate meeting within 48 hours. |
| **WARM** | Open to reconnecting, said "let me check my schedule" or "sounds good" | **ACTIVE** — Follow up within 3-5 days to confirm meeting. |
| **COOL** | Polite but non-committal, "maybe sometime" or no clear yes | **MONTHLY** — One re-engagement attempt per month, max 3 months. |
| **NOT NOW** | Gave a specific reason or timeline ("busy this month," "call me in May") | **SCHEDULED** — Follow up at their stated time. |
| **NO RESPONSE** | Completed full cadence with zero response | **PARK** — Wait 2-3 months, try one more outreach. |
| **DECLINED** | Clearly said not interested or do not contact | **CLOSE** — Log reason. Do not re-engage unless Jack instructs otherwise. |

## Initial Outreach Sequence (Day 1 through Day 21)

### Day 1 — Phone Call (Attempt 1)

- **Channel:** RingCentral
- **Action:** Call the contact. Use Gatekeeper Script or Direct Contact Script.
- **If connected:** Follow the warm reconnection script. Capture details. Classify the contact.
- **If voicemail:** Leave Voicemail 1. Log in tracker.
- **If no answer / no voicemail:** Log as "No Answer."
- **Same day (within 2 hours):** Send Email Template 1 (whether call connected or not)

### Day 3 — Phone Call (Attempt 2)

- **Channel:** RingCentral
- **Action:** Second call attempt
- **If voicemail:** Leave Voicemail 2 (shorter, reference first call and email)
- **If no answer:** Log. Do NOT leave a third voicemail.

### Day 7 — Follow-Up Email

- **Channel:** Outlook (khalifehassociates.com email)
- **Action:** Send Email Template 2 (gentle follow-up)
- **Only send if:** No response to the initial email

### Day 14 — SMS (If Still No Response)

- **Channel:** RingCentral or personal phone
- **Action:** Send SMS Template (one-time text message)
- **Only send if:** No response to 2 calls + 2 emails

### Day 21 — Final Email

- **Channel:** Outlook
- **Action:** Send Email Template 3 (final gentle touch)
- **Only send if:** Still no response after all previous attempts

### After the Initial Sequence — Classify & Route

| Outcome | Classification | Next Step |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| Enthusiastic, wants to meet | **HOT** | Coordinate meeting immediately |
| Open but hasn't committed | **WARM** | Follow Warm Lead cadence |
| Polite, non-committal | **COOL** | Wait 1 month, try once more |
| Gave a specific timeline | **NOT NOW** | Set calendar reminder for their date |
| No response after full 21-day sequence | **NO RESPONSE** | Park for 2-3 months |
| Said "not interested" / "don't call" | **DECLINED** | Close. Log reason. |

## HOT Contact Cadence

**These contacts are ready to meet. Your job: get the meeting on the calendar FAST.**

| When | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Within 1 hour of call** | Notify Jack (WhatsApp or in-person). Share all details. |
| **Same day** | Send confirmation email to the contact with 2-3 proposed dates/times |
| **Next day** | If no response to date proposal, call to confirm |
| **Within 48 hours** | Meeting should be confirmed and on Jack's calendar |
| **Day before meeting** | Send reminder to the contact: "Looking forward to lunch tomorrow at [place] at [time]." |
| **Day after meeting** | Send thank-you email from Fiona on Jack's behalf |

### Meeting Coordination Checklist

- [ ] Confirm date and time with both Jack and the contact
- [ ] Ask the contact for restaurant preference
- [ ] If no preference, suggest 2-3 of Jack's preferred restaurants
- [ ] Add to Jack's calendar with full details (who, where, context/talking points)
- [ ] Prepare a one-page brief for Jack: contact background, last project together, what they said on the call, any concerns/opportunities
- [ ] Day-before reminder to the contact
- [ ] Day-after thank-you email

## WARM Contact Cadence

**These contacts are open but haven't committed to a meeting yet. Keep the connection warm.**

| Timing | Channel | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **3-5 days after initial call** | Phone | Quick check-in: "Hi [Name], just following up on our conversation — were you able to check your schedule?" |
| **1 week later** | Email | Friendly nudge: "Jack is flexible on dates — happy to work around your calendar." |
| **2 weeks later** | Phone | Brief check-in: "Just wanted to see if there's a better time in the coming weeks." |
| **3 weeks later** | Email | Light touch with value: Share something relevant (industry news, project milestone, Jack's LinkedIn post) |
| **After 4 weeks with no progress** | — | **Transition to Monthly** |

## Monthly Re-Engagement (Cool / No Response)

| Timing | Channel | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **1 month after last contact** | Phone | Brief, warm call: "Hi [Name], it's Fiona from Jack's office. Just checking in to see how things are going. Jack would still love to catch up whenever works for you." |
| **Same day** | Email | Short follow-up: "Great to touch base — whenever the timing is right, Jack would love to get together." |
| **2 months after** | Email | Light touch: "Hope all is well. Jack sends his regards." |
| **3 months after** | Phone | Final attempt: "Hi [Name], it's been a little while. Jack wanted me to reach out one more time..." |

### After 3 Monthly Attempts with No Response:

- **Park the contact.** Do not continue monthly outreach.
- Set a 6-month reminder to try once more.
- If still no response after the 6-month attempt, mark as Dormant. Only re-engage if Jack specifically asks.

## Channel Rules

- After every call (connected or voicemail): Send a follow-up email within 2 hours
- Never call more than 2x without sending an email in between
- Never email more than 2x without calling in between
- SMS only after call + email cycle with no response
- Only 1 SMS per contact (unless they respond)
- Alternate channels to avoid feeling repetitive
- **LinkedIn messages:** Can be used for contacts where we only have LinkedIn (Julie Smith-Clemente research phase). Not a primary channel.

## When to STOP Following Up

**Stop and close out the contact if:**
- They explicitly said "I'm not interested in reconnecting" or "Do not contact me"
- Jack tells you to stop
- You've completed the full 3-month cadence + 6-month follow-up with zero response

**When in doubt:** Ask Jack. Include in your weekly report: "Should I continue reaching out to [Name]? Here's what's happened so far..."
