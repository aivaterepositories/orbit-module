## Purpose

This document defines exactly how the VA passes qualified leads to Nick Kavali. The handoff must be **fast, clean, and complete** — Nick should never have to chase down missing details or guess what happened on the call.

**Primary handoff method: EMAIL.** Nick does not carry the Verizon OneTalk number on his cell phone, so live call transfers are NOT the standard process. All handoffs go through email, with Shelly Evans CC'd on every communication.

## Lead Types — Know What You're Working With

| Type | Definition | What It Looks Like | Handoff Speed |
|------|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| **HOT** | Actively interested. Wants a tour, asks about pricing, wants to talk to Nick, or says "we're looking for space now" | "We'd love to tour tomorrow" / "How much is the rent?" / "Can I talk to the property manager?" | **IMMEDIATE** — Email Nick within minutes of hanging up |
| **WARM** | Interested but not ready to act. Wants info, says "maybe later," asks questions, or says "send me details" | "Send me an email" / "I need to check with my partner" / "Sounds interesting, but not right now" | **SAME DAY** — Include in daily lead report |
| **COOL** | Polite but non-committal. No clear interest, but didn't shut the door | "I'll think about it" / Generic politeness without engagement | **DAILY REPORT** — Log and include in weekly review |
| **COLD** | Not interested. Clear no, wrong fit, or disqualified | "We just signed a 5-year lease" / "We're too small" / "Not our area" | **LOG ONLY** — Note WHY they said no. Do not re-engage unless circumstances change. |
| **FUTURE** | Not ready now, but gave a timeline or lease expiry date | "Our lease is up in 18 months" / "Check back with me in the fall" | **SCHEDULED** — Set reminder 3–6 months before their stated timeline |
| **DISQUALIFIED** | Clearly not a fit AND no future potential | Business is closing, completely wrong industry with no office needs, hostile/abusive | **LOG & CLOSE** — Remove from active list. Do not spend more VA time. |

### Key Principle from Nick:
> Office space is a unique product. Some businesses just signed a 5-year lease — continuing to call them wastes precious VA time. Capture WHY they're a no and move on. Focus your energy on leads that have a realistic chance of converting.

## The Handoff — Step by Step

### HOT Lead Handoff (Immediate)

**When:** You just had a conversation where the lead is actively interested in the space.

1. **Hang up the call** (do not try to live transfer — Nick may not be available)
2. **Within 5 minutes**, send an email to Nick (CC Shelly):

**Subject:** HOT LEAD — [Business Name] — [Contact Name]

> Hi Nick,
>
> I just got off a call with **[Contact Name]**, **[Title]** at **[Business Name]**. This is a hot lead.
>
> **Summary:** [2-3 sentences about what was discussed, what they're interested in, their tone]
>
> **Key Details:**
> - Phone: [number]
> - Email: [if captured]
> - Space interest: [Suite 200 / Suite 240 / Combined / General inquiry]
> - Timeline: [e.g., "Looking to move in within 60 days" or "Wants to tour this week"]
> - Availability: [e.g., "Available any day next week" or "Best reached mornings"]
>
> **Suggested next step:** [e.g., "They'd like a tour — please contact them directly to schedule."]
>
> Best,
> [VA Name]

3. **Log in tracker** — Mark as HOT, set follow-up for tomorrow to confirm Nick reached out
4. **Do NOT tell the lead you'll schedule a tour** — Nick handles his own calendar

### WARM Lead Handoff (Same-Day)

**When:** Lead showed interest but isn't ready to act now.

1. **Send the follow-up email** to the lead (the email template from the Follow-Up Cadence doc)
2. **Log in tracker** — Mark as WARM with full notes
3. **Include in your daily lead report** to Nick (CC Shelly) at end of shift

### COOL / COLD / FUTURE Lead Handoff (Daily Report)

**When:** Lead was polite, not interested, or gave a future timeline.

1. **Log in tracker** with full notes, including WHY they're not interested
2. **Include in daily lead report** under the appropriate section
3. **Set follow-up dates** in tracker:
   - COOL: Re-engage in 1 month
   - COLD: Close out (no follow-up unless Nick says otherwise)
   - FUTURE: Set reminder based on their stated timeline

## Good Handoff vs. Bad Handoff

### GOOD Handoff Example

**Subject:** HOT LEAD — Brightline Legal Group — Sarah Chen

> Hi Nick,
>
> I just got off a call with **Sarah Chen**, **Office Manager** at **Brightline Legal Group**. This is a hot lead.
>
> **Summary:** Sarah's firm currently has 12 attorneys and is outgrowing their current space on Holcomb Bridge. Their lease expires in August. She was very engaged and asked detailed questions about the building. She specifically asked about Suite 200 because they need private offices for their attorneys plus a conference room setup.
>
> **Key Details:**
> - Phone: (770) 555-1234
> - Email: sarah.chen@brightlinelegal.com
> - Space interest: Suite 200 (needs private offices + conference rooms)
> - Timeline: Lease up in August, wants to tour within the next 2 weeks
> - Availability: Tuesday and Thursday afternoons work best for her
>
> **Suggested next step:** She'd like a tour. Please contact her directly to schedule.
>
> Best,
> [VA Name]

**Why this is good:**
- Nick knows exactly who to call and why
- He has the phone number, email, and best times to reach her
- He knows what suite to focus on and why
- He knows the timeline and urgency
- He can prep for the conversation (law firm, 12 attorneys, Suite 200)

---

### BAD Handoff Example

**Subject:** Lead

> Hi Nick,
>
> I talked to someone at a law firm today. They seemed interested. Their number is (770) 555-1234.
>
> [VA Name]

**Why this is bad:**
- No business name — Nick doesn't know who he's calling
- No contact name — Nick has to ask "who did I speak with?"
- "Seemed interested" — how? What did they say? What are they interested in?
- No email — if the call doesn't go through, Nick has no backup channel
- No space preference — Nick doesn't know which suite to talk about
- No timeline — Nick doesn't know the urgency
- No next step — what is Nick supposed to do with this?

---

### BAD Handoff Example #2 (Over-promising)

**Subject:** TOUR SCHEDULED — ABC Corp

> Hi Nick,
>
> Great news! I scheduled a tour with ABC Corp for this Thursday at 10am. They'll be bringing their whole team.
>
> [VA Name]

**Why this is bad:**
- **VA should NEVER schedule tours.** Nick manages his own calendar.
- Nick might have a conflict Thursday at 10am
- Nick might be at another property dealing with an emergency
- Now Nick has to either cancel (bad look) or rearrange his day
- The correct approach: "They'd like a tour this week. Please contact them to schedule."

## What to Say on the Call When Routing to Nick

### If the lead asks to speak with Nick directly:

> "I'd love to get you connected with Nick. He's the best person to walk you through the details and answer any specific questions. Let me get your contact information and I'll have him reach out to you directly — he's very responsive on email and phone."

### If you can't answer a question:

> "That's a great question — I want to make sure I give you the most accurate answer. Let me check with Nick and get back to you shortly. What's the best way to reach you?"

Then: Email Nick with the question, get the answer, follow up with the lead.

### If the lead wants a tour:

> "That's great — Nick would love to show you the space. Let me grab your contact info and availability, and I'll have him reach out to coordinate a time that works."

Then: Send HOT lead email to Nick with availability details. Nick schedules directly.

### What NOT to say:

- "Let me transfer you to Nick right now" — he may not be available and it looks bad
- "I can schedule you for Thursday at 2pm" — you don't manage Nick's calendar
- "The rent is $19.50 per square foot" — you don't discuss specific pricing
- "Sure, I can negotiate on the rate" — absolutely not

## Flowchart: Lead Handoff Decision Tree

```
CALL ENDS
    │
    ├── Lead is ACTIVELY INTERESTED (wants tour, pricing, to talk to Nick)
    │       → Send HOT LEAD email to Nick + Shelly within 5 minutes
    │       → Log in tracker as HOT
    │       → DO NOT schedule tour — Nick handles that
    │
    ├── Lead is INTERESTED but not urgent (wants email, "maybe later")
    │       → Send follow-up email to the lead
    │       → Log in tracker as WARM
    │       → Include in daily lead report
    │
    ├── Lead shared LEASE EXPIRY DATE or said "check back later"
    │       → Log as FUTURE with the date
    │       → Set follow-up reminder (3-6 months before)
    │       → Include in daily report
    │
    ├── Lead is NOT INTERESTED but polite
    │       → Log as COOL or COLD with reason
    │       → COOL: Re-engage in 1 month
    │       → COLD: Close out
    │
    ├── Lead is CLEARLY DISQUALIFIED (wrong fit, no potential)
    │       → Log reason. Close out. Move on.
    │
    ├── NO ANSWER / VOICEMAIL
    │       → Log attempt. Continue cadence sequence.
    │
    └── You CAN'T ANSWER a question
            → Tell lead: "Let me check and get back to you"
            → Email Nick the question
            → Follow up with lead once you have the answer
```
