## Discovery Questions — Contact Research

### A. Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

1. **Who is your ideal prospect?** What type of commercial real estate client are you targeting — tenants looking for space, property owners/landlords, investors, developers?
2. **What property types do you focus on?** (Office, retail, industrial, multifamily, land, mixed-use?)
3. **What geographic area do you work?** Is it local to a specific region, statewide, or national?
4. **What's the typical deal size or property value range you're going after?**
5. **Is there a specific title or role you want the VA to target?** (e.g., business owners, office managers, property managers, CFOs, developers?)
6. **Are there any industries, company sizes, or prospect types you do NOT want targeted?**
7. **Can you show me an example of 2-3 recent clients or deals that represent your "ideal"?** What made them a good fit?

### B. Research Process & Sources

8. **Where do you currently find your prospects?** (LinkedIn, CoStar, LoopNet, local business directories, referrals, driving for dollars, networking events?)
9. **Do you use any tools for contact research or email verification today?** (ZoomInfo, Apollo, Hunter.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator?)
10. **What specific data points does the VA need to find for each contact?**
    - Full name
    - Title/role
    - Company name
    - Phone (direct/mobile)
    - Email (verified)
    - LinkedIn URL
    - Property/space details (if applicable)
    - Any others?
11. **How do you verify that a contact's email or phone is valid before reaching out?** Do you want the VA to verify, or just find and log?
12. **How many new contacts per day or per week do you expect the VA to research?** Do you have a target, or should we figure that out together?

### C. Pipedrive & Data Management

> **[ASK BRAYDEN TO SCREEN-SHARE PIPEDRIVE HERE]**

13. **Walk me through your Pipedrive setup.** What pipeline stages do you currently have? What do they mean in your workflow?
14. **When the VA finds a new contact, where exactly in Pipedrive should they add it?** (Contacts? Leads? Deals? Which pipeline?)
15. **What fields need to be filled in when adding a new contact?** (Let's look at the fields together on screen)
16. **Are there any tags, labels, or filters you currently use — or want to use — to segment contacts?** (e.g., by property type, lead source, geography, deal stage)
17. **After a contact is logged in Pipedrive, what happens next?** Does the VA immediately proceed to outreach, or does it sit until you review it, or does it enter an automated sequence?
18. **Are there any Pipedrive automations currently set up?** (Even basic ones — email triggers, activity reminders, stage-change actions?)
19. **Do you use any integrations with Pipedrive?** (Email sync, calendar sync, web forms, Zapier, etc.)
20. **Who else has access to Pipedrive?** Just you, or does Hayes or anyone else use it?

### D. Quality & Workflow

21. **Do you want to review/approve contacts before the VA does anything with them, or can the VA add and move forward independently?**
22. **What does a "bad" contact look like?** What would be a waste of time for the VA to log?
23. **How should the VA flag duplicates or contacts that are already in Pipedrive?**
24. **How would you like the VA to report on research activity?** (End-of-day summary? Weekly count? Pipedrive activity log?)
