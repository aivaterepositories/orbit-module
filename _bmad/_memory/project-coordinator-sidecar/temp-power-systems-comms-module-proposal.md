# TPS — Internal Communication Module (Proposed)

**Status:** Proposal — Awaiting Cob & Marky feasibility input

---

## Concept

Internal communication section within the TPS CRM. Replaces reliance on email and text messaging with centralized, job-linked conversations visible to all relevant team members.

## Problem Statement

- TPS team communicates via email and text — conversations are siloed
- Hard to trace conversations back to specific jobs or work orders
- Poor visibility — team members can't see exchanges they weren't part of
- Information gets lost across inboxes and text threads

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Scope | Price |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Standard** | Basic messaging interface within CRM, tied to jobs/work orders, visible to relevant team members | ~$2,000 |
| **Advanced** | Standard + AI-powered conversation summaries per job, email/SMS platform integrations, smarter context linking | $3,000–$4,000 |

Final pricing depends on technical complexity assessment from Cob and Marky.
