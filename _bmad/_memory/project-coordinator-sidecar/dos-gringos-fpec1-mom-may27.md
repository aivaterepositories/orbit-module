## Key Findings

### 1. GHL Pipeline State — "Work in Progress"

Walker opened by saying the pipelines are actively being restructured. Three parties are influencing GHL changes simultaneously:
- **Walker & team** — internal cleanup
- **Aivate/Fiona** — process engineering for VA
- **Roar (marketing agency)** — will have opinions on follow-up automations since lead gen and lead management go hand-in-hand

**Fiona's recommendation:** Document what exists today. When the restructuring settles, schedule another engineering call to re-document.

### 2. Two Pipelines Confirmed

| Pipeline | Priority | State |
|----------|----------|-------|
| **PPC Leads** | #1 (highest cost per lead, always primary) | Cleaner, automations work better, fewer leads |
| **Outbound Lead Pipeline** | #2 | Messy, cluttered with stale leads, automations not optimized for cold calling style |

### 3. Outbound Pipeline Stages (Current)

| Stage | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **New Lead** | Fresh lead, nobody has spoken to them yet. Utmost priority when they arrive. |
| **Not Engaged, Follow Up** | Called but didn't get through. Automations send drip follow-up texts. |
| **Engaged** | Answered or showed interest. Initial interaction happened — "fish on the hook." All drip automations removed once moved here. |
| **Triaged** | Script has been presented. Received a phone call, consultation, or in-person meeting. Cash or creative offer extended. |
| **Warm** | Good opportunity identified after triage. |
| **Perfect Presentation** | Very warm, ready to go, highest confidence. |
| **Under Contract** | Closed deals. |
| **Long-Term Follow-Up** | Want to sell but timeline is 6+ months out. |
| **Dead / Lost / Undesirable** | Don't want to be contacted anymore. |

### 4. PPC Pipeline

- Simpler and more functional than outbound
- Immediate text sent when a lead submits a form (e.g., "Thanks for contacting us" — sent even at 3:30 AM)
- Speed-to-lead = making the call as soon as possible during business hours
- Walker noted: "The PPC pipeline has a pretty natural flow with the workflows"

### 5. Automations — Current State

**What exists:**
- PPC: Immediate auto-text on form submission
- Outbound: Series of follow-up texts when lead is in "Not Engaged" (e.g., "Did I do something wrong? I haven't heard back from you...")
- When lead moves to "Engaged" → all drip follow-up tags are removed (human takes over)

**Problem discovered (and fixed):**
- Automation kept texting leads even after they responded — looked unprofessional ("they think we're some robot")
- Walker's team fixed: Now, when a lead responds with any answer, automation turns off and team is notified for manual follow-up

**Walker's concern:** Outbound automations were designed for PPC-style leads. They don't fit cold calling workflows well. Needs rethinking.

### 6. VA Work Priority (Confirmed)

**Pipeline priority:** PPC first, always.

**Within each pipeline — stage priority:**
1. **New Leads** — utmost priority when they come in (speed-to-lead)
2. **Perfect Presentation** → **Warm** — leads ready to sell
3. **Triaged** → **Engaged** → **Not Engaged** — work backwards

**Walker's philosophy for the VA:** "I don't want them to be another cold caller. I want them to be speaking to leads that are ready to go right now."

### 7. Lead Volume

- Current: 5–7 leads/day (10/day is not a regular occurrence yet)
- Planned: 7–10/day once Roar partnership ramps up

### 8. Task Management Feature — NEW

- GHL has a built-in task management feature Walker wasn't using
- Lane Anderson (acquisitions team member acting as pseudo lead manager) had been creating tasks — 702 tasks exist, poorly managed, many overdue
- Fiona walked Walker through creating tasks:
  - From the Tasks section in Contacts → Add Task → assign to person → link to contact
  - From the Conversations tab → select a contact → task button on right side → add task (auto-linked to contact)
- **Decision:** VA will use task management for daily organization ("What are my tasks? Who do I call back today?")

### 9. Notes/Documentation — NEW

- Notes section on contact cards for internal documentation
- Some team members (outbound) already using notes; PPC leads had no notes
- **Decision:** VA must document everything on each lead's notes section — keeps acquisitions team informed for handoff

### 10. Separate VA Pipeline — REJECTED

Walker proposed creating a new pipeline exclusively for VA-managed leads to measure VA performance separately. Fiona recommended against it:
- Too much to set up right now
- Instead: implement SOD/EOD reports for visibility into VA activity
- Walker agreed: "Let's just do it that way"

### 11. SOD/EOD Report & Daily Sync — AGREED

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Start of Day Report** | VA identifies priority leads for the day, lists who they'll contact |
| **End of Day Report** | Summary of what happened, outcomes, next-day plan |
| **Daily Sync** | 30 min morning standup with Walker — can be a phone call (Walker can be driving/walking). Plan the day, set expectations, pass critical info. |
| **Walker's preference** | Morning sync preferred |

### 12. All Calls Recorded

- All calls made through GHL are recorded and playable from the contact card
- VA calls will also be recorded — useful for QA and training

### 13. Walker's GHL Knowledge

- Walker admitted: "I really do not know a lot about GoHighLevel as a system"
- Open to learning and implementing recommendations
