The 15-Minute Playbook

Your minute-by-minute game plan for every meeting
▼
0:00 – 1:30
The Warm Open
Be human. Break the screen barrier.
"Hi [Name]! I've been looking forward to this one. Speed dating for government contracts — who knew this would be my Wednesday afternoon?"
"Hi! I'm Fiona from Khalifeh & Associates. These back-to-back meetings can feel like a marathon, so I'll keep it fun."
1:30 – 4:00
Let Them Go First
Show respect. People love talking about what they do.
"Before I jump in — I'd love to hear about your role and what you're focused on right now. What's keeping you busy?"
Listen for:
  • Types of projects or contracts they manage
  • Facility design, construction, or engineering mentions
  • Small business goals or subcontracting needs
  • Upcoming projects or pain points
4:00 – 6:00
Your Intro (Tailored)
Deliver the pitch, but weave in what they just told you.
  • If they mentioned facilities: "That's exactly our lane."
  • If they mentioned SB goals: "We're a small business MEP firm, so that's music to our ears."
  • If they mentioned a project type: "We've done exactly that — [reference KFA project]."
6:00 – 10:00
Discovery — Pre-Qualify Them
Find out if there's a real opportunity.
The Three Big Questions:
  1. "What does it take for a firm like ours to get into your network / vendor database?"
  2. "What types of MEP work do you contract out — and is there anything on the horizon?"
  3. "Is there a registration portal or specific process we should go through?"
10:00 – 13:00
The Ask
Plant the seed for a follow-up with Jack.
"I'd love to continue this beyond today's speed round. Our principal, Jack Khalifeh, has 40 years in this business and would love to connect. Would you be open to a longer call — or even a lunch if you're ever in the LA area?"
  • Very interested: Offer to have Jack reach out directly
  • Moderate: Offer to send the KFA profile
  • Warm but noncommittal: Ask for email, send follow-up
13:00 – 15:00
Exchange Info + Warm Exit
Get their contact. Leave them smiling.
"What's the best email to send our profile to?"
"This was great — I'm glad they matched us up. Enjoy the rest of your speed dates!"
