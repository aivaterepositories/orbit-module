## SLIDE 3: Understanding RNR Property Solutions

### What to Cover
Present what you already know from the RFP, then validate and expand:

**What we know:**
- Real estate investment company — property management + fix-and-flip + new construction
- Haruna (COO) and Willie (Co-owner/Operator)
- Local market (hands-on) + Central Florida (remote-managed)
- Current team: Ozbell (PM, local ops), existing VAs (admin), contractors
- Business model: PM + project management + capital raising

### Questions to Ask

**Business Structure & Scale**

1. How many rental properties are you currently managing?
2. How many are in the local market vs. Central Florida?
3. How many fix-and-flip or new construction projects are active right now?
4. What's the split of your time between property management, flips, and capital raising?
5. What does your revenue model look like — is PM the steady income and flips are the big paydays?

*Why this matters: Determines the volume the VA will handle and whether the role is sized correctly for full-time.*

**Team & Roles**

6. Willie — walk me through a typical day for you. What's the first thing you do in the morning, and what does the rest of the day look like?
7. Haruna — what's your day-to-day involvement in the PM side vs. the business side?
8. Ozbell — what do you handle on the ground? Where does your responsibility start and stop?
9. Tell me about your current VAs — how many, what hours, what do they handle today?
10. Where does the current VA support end and where should this new PM VA begin? Is there overlap?
11. Is anyone on the team handling PM tasks that should be moved to this new VA?

*Why this matters: Feeds directly into the role design and the communication flow map — who does what, who approves what, who escalates to whom.*

**Central Florida Operations**

12. How are the Central Florida projects managed remotely today? Who's the boots on the ground there?
13. Will the new VA support Central Florida properties, or is this strictly local?
14. If both — how do the workflows differ between local and remote-managed properties?

*Why this matters: If the VA covers both markets, the SOPs need to account for remote vs. local workflow differences.*

**Growth & Future State**

15. Where do you want to be in 12 months — more rentals, more flips, or both?
16. Is the goal to eventually have this VA handle all PM so Willie can focus entirely on flips and capital raising?
17. Are you planning to bring on more VAs after this one?

*Why this matters: Shapes whether we build the playbook for a single VA or design it to be scalable for a future team.*

## SLIDE 5: Property Management VA Role + Systems & Tools Audit

### Part A: The Role

**What to Cover:**
Present the proposed role — Property Management VA (primary) with secondary project management support. Walk through the core responsibility areas and get their reaction.

### Questions — Role Design

23. Does this feel like the right role? Is there anything you'd add or take away?
24. How do you envision the handoff between this VA and your existing VAs? Who owns what?
25. Does the VA need to be bilingual (English/Spanish) given the Spanish-speaking tenant segment?
26. What working hours do you need this VA online? Does it need to overlap with your schedule, Ozbell's, or both?
27. How do you want to communicate with the VA day-to-day — WhatsApp, Slack, email, something else?
28. What level of autonomy do you want this VA to have from Day 1 vs. after a ramp period?
   - Can they respond to tenant inquiries without approval?
   - Can they schedule maintenance without checking with Willie/Ozbell first?
   - Can they send late payment notices on their own?
29. Who is the VA's direct supervisor — Willie, Haruna, or both?
30. What does "good performance" look like in 90 days? What would make you say "this is working"?

*Why this matters: Autonomy levels feed directly into the SOPs — every process needs a clear "VA does this independently" vs. "VA needs approval" boundary.*

### Part B: Systems & Tools Audit

**What to Cover:**
Present what you know about their tech stack, then fill in every gap. You need the COMPLETE picture to build the Tool Workflow Guide (Deliverable #1).

**Known tools:**
- TurboTenant (listings, screening, rent collection, maintenance, lease management)
- Zillow Rentals (listing syndication)
- Facebook Marketplace (listings)
- Furnished Finders (rent-by-room)
- Monday.com (project management)
- CRM (tenant/vendor calls and texts — name TBD)
- WhatsApp (contractors, Spanish-speaking tenants)
- Email (documents, payment instructions)
- Wells Fargo (banking)
- Google Drive (document storage)
- Google Docs (notices)
- IDIcore (background checks)
- Rentometer (rental comps)
- Property Inspection App (move-in/move-out — name TBD)

### Questions — Tools & Systems

31. **CRM:** What CRM are you using? Is it the same system for tenants AND vendors, or separate? What's it called?
32. **Monday.com:** Walk me through your Monday.com setup — is it for flips only, PM too, or both? What boards/workflows do you have?
33. **TurboTenant:** Is TurboTenant your central hub for PM, or do things live in multiple places? Are all your properties set up in it?
34. **Listing sync:** Does TurboTenant auto-sync to Zillow, or do you manually post to each platform?
35. **Phone system:** Do tenants call a main business number, or personal cell phones? What system — RingCentral, Google Voice, other?
36. **Property Inspection App:** What app do you use for move-in/move-out inspections? Is it connected to anything else?
37. **Accounting/Bookkeeping:** Do you use any accounting software — QuickBooks, Wave, Stessa, spreadsheets? How do you track expenses per property?
38. **Document storage:** Is Google Drive the main file storage? How is it organized — by property, by year, by type?
39. **E-signatures:** Are you using TurboTenant's built-in e-signature, or a separate tool (DocuSign, HelloSign)?
40. **Payment channels:** You mentioned Zelle, ACH, bank transfers — are tenants also paying through TurboTenant directly, or is it all external?
41. **Passwords & access:** Do you use a password manager? How will you share tool access with the VA securely?
42. **Anything else?** Are there any tools, apps, or platforms I haven't mentioned that you use regularly — even if they seem minor?

*Why this matters: This is the foundation for Deliverable #1 (Tool Workflow Guide). Every tool needs to be mapped — what it does, who uses it, how it connects to the next tool in the workflow.*

### Listen For (Don't Ask Directly)

- Are they using tools that overlap (e.g., tracking maintenance in both TurboTenant AND Google Sheets)?
- Are there manual steps that could be automated (e.g., manually posting to Zillow when TurboTenant can sync)?
- Is information siloed — does Ozbell know things that Willie doesn't because it lives in WhatsApp?
- Are there tools they're paying for but barely using (Monday.com is a common one)?

## SLIDE 6: Deliverables & Timeline

### What to Cover
Walk through each deliverable in order. For each one, explain briefly what it is and why it matters. This is also your chance to ask any remaining questions that tie to specific deliverables.

### Deliverable 1: Tool Workflow Guide
*How each tool connects — TurboTenant, Zillow, Monday.com, CRM, WhatsApp*

**What you're building:** A visual/written map showing which tool is used for what, how data flows between them, and where the VA operates day-to-day.

**Questions (if not already covered):**

43. When a new lead comes in on Zillow, what happens next? Walk me through the exact steps until they become a tenant.
44. When a maintenance request comes in on TurboTenant, does it stay there or does someone move it to Monday.com or a spreadsheet?
45. Is there any data that has to be manually moved from one tool to another (copy-paste, re-entry)?

### Deliverable 2: Property Management Process (End-to-End SOPs)
*Full lifecycle SOPs for every PM workflow*

**What you're building:** Enhanced, detailed SOPs that go beyond the draft they provided — with approval gates, escalation paths, timelines, and edge cases.

**Questions:**

46. For tenant screening — who makes the final approval decision? Willie, Haruna, or both?
47. Your SOP says credit score 620+ but "flexible based on income" — what does flexible look like? Is there a floor?
48. For lease agreements — who reviews the lease before it goes out? Is there a lawyer involved, or does Willie sign off?
49. For the welcome packet — what's in it? Is there a digital version, or is it always physical?
50. For late payments — who issues the 3-Day Pay or Quit notice? Is that a legal document? Who drafts it?
51. For security deposit deductions — who decides what's deducted? Is there a standard deduction list?
52. **Edge cases:** What happens when something goes wrong that's NOT in the SOP? Give me an example of a situation that caught you off guard.
53. Are there any state or local regulations specific to Florida that the VA needs to be aware of? (Fair housing, eviction process, deposit return timelines, etc.)

### Deliverable 3: Communication Flow Map
*Defined channels, response templates, and escalation paths across WhatsApp, email, CRM*

**What you're building:** A clear map of WHO communicates with WHOM, through WHICH channel, for WHAT purpose, and what happens when a message needs to be escalated.

**Questions:**

54. If a tenant has an emergency at 10 PM — what happens? Who do they contact and through what channel?
55. When the VA gets a tenant message they can't answer, what's the escalation path? Who do they go to first — Willie, Haruna, or Ozbell?
56. For Spanish-speaking tenants — does the VA need to respond in Spanish, or does Ozbell handle that?
57. How quickly do tenants expect a response? What's acceptable — 1 hour, same day, 24 hours?
58. Do you have any existing response templates or canned messages? (The SOP mentions one for inquiry responses — are there others?)
59. How do you want contractor/vendor communication handled — does the VA communicate with them directly, or always through Ozbell?
60. Is there a group chat or team channel where the team communicates internally? Or is it all 1-on-1?

### Deliverable 4: Maintenance Coordination Process
*Triage system (Emergency/Urgent/Routine), vendor coordination, tracking and follow-up*

**What you're building:** A structured triage and dispatch system with clear definitions, response time targets, and a tracking workflow from request to resolution.

**Questions:**

61. What counts as an Emergency vs. Urgent vs. Routine? Can you give me an example of each?
62. Your SOP says Ozbell is the main POC for all maintenance — does that mean the VA always goes through Ozbell, or can the VA dispatch contractors directly for routine items?
63. Do you have a preferred vendor/contractor list? How many contractors do you work with regularly?
64. How are estimates approved? Is there a dollar threshold — e.g., under $200 just do it, over $200 needs Willie's approval?
65. How do you currently track open vs. closed maintenance requests? Is TurboTenant the source of truth, or are things also tracked in Google Sheets/Monday.com?
66. What's the follow-up process — does someone check back with the tenant after a repair? How soon?
67. How do you handle repeat issues (e.g., same plumbing problem three times)?

### Deliverable 5: Daily Reporting Structure
*Start-of-day and end-of-day report templates and cadence*

**What you're building:** A daily reporting template the VA fills out to keep the team informed — what was done, what's pending, what needs attention.

**Questions:**

68. The RFP mentions daily start-of-day and end-of-day reports — is that something you already do, or is that aspirational?
69. Who should these reports go to — Willie, Haruna, or both?
70. What format — email, Slack message, Monday.com update, shared Google Doc?
71. What do you want to see in a daily report? (Tasks completed, pending items, escalations, tenant interactions, payments received?)
72. How about a weekly summary — would that be valuable on top of dailies?
73. Do your existing VAs report daily? If so, what does that look like and what's missing?

### Deliverable 6: Refined VA Job Description
*Based on IPEC findings and process engineering*

**What you're building:** A detailed, realistic JD that reflects what we've designed today — ready for the placements team to source against.

**Questions:**

74. The rate is $12/hr full-time — are you flexible on that if the right candidate needs $13–14, or is $12 the ceiling?
75. Does this VA need property management experience specifically, or is general real estate/admin experience acceptable?
76. How important is bilingual (English/Spanish)?
77. Have you worked with VAs before for property management? What worked and what didn't?
78. Are there any personality traits or soft skills that are non-negotiable for you? (e.g., proactive communicator, detail-oriented, comfortable with ambiguity)
79. What timezone do you need this VA working in?

### Deliverable 7: VA Selection & Onboarding Support
*Screening criteria, interview support, onboarding checklist*

**What you're building:** A screening rubric + interview question bank + first-week onboarding plan.

**Questions:**

80. Do you want to be involved in the final interview, or do you trust the placements team + Fiona to shortlist?
81. Who on your team will be onboarding the VA — Willie, Haruna, or someone else?
82. How much training time are you willing to invest in the first 2 weeks?
83. What access will the VA need on Day 1? (TurboTenant, CRM, email, WhatsApp, Monday.com, bank view-only?)
84. Is there a probation period or performance review milestone you want to set?
