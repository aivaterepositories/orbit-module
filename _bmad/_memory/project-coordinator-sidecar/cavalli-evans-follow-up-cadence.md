## Overview

This document defines the exact follow-up rhythm for every prospect the VA contacts. The cadence is designed to be **persistent but not aggressive** — Nick's strong preference is to avoid bombarding prospects.

**Key principle:** Office leasing is a unique product. Unlike gym subscriptions or SaaS, prospects may have signed a 5-year lease. Continuing to call them wastes precious VA time. Capture why they said no, note any relevant details (like lease expiry), and move on. Focus energy on leads with realistic conversion potential.

---

## Lead Categories

| Category | Definition | Follow-Up Priority |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **HOT** | Actively looking for space, wants a tour, asked about pricing, or wants to talk to Nick | **IMMEDIATE** — Email Nick within 5 minutes |
| **WARM** | Showed interest, requested email, asked questions, or said "not now but maybe later" | **SAME DAY** — Include in daily report, follow up in cadence |
| **COOL** | Polite but non-committal, no clear interest, didn't fully shut the door | **MONTHLY** — Re-engage once after 1 month |
| **COLD** | Not interested, clearly not a fit, or said a firm no | **DROP** — Log reason, close out. Do not re-engage unless Nick says otherwise |
| **FUTURE** | Has a lease expiring or gave a specific timeline ("check back in 6 months") | **SCHEDULED** — Follow up 3–6 months before their stated date |
| **DISQUALIFIED** | Clearly wrong fit with zero future potential | **CLOSE** — Remove from active list |

## Initial Outreach Sequence (Day 1 through Day 12)

### Day 1 — Phone Call (Attempt 1)

- **Channel:** Verizon OneTalk
- **Action:** Call the prospect. Use Gatekeeper Script or Principal Contact Script
- **If connected:** Follow the cold call script. Capture info. Classify the lead.
- **If voicemail:** Leave Voicemail 1. Log in tracker.
- **If no answer / no voicemail:** Log as "No Answer."
- **IMMEDIATELY AFTER (within 2 hours):** Send follow-up email to the lead

**Post-Call Email (sent same day whether call connected or not):**

> **Subject:** Office Space Opportunity in Roswell — [Business Name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for taking my call earlier today *(or: I tried reaching you by phone today)*.
>
> My name is [VA Name] and I'm reaching out on behalf of Nick Kavali at WePartner Commercial. We manage Mansell Office Park, a professional office building located at the corner of Mansell Rd and Houze Rd in Roswell, GA — less than 2 miles from GA-400.
>
> We currently have move-in ready office suites available at 695 Mansell Rd:
> - **Suite 200** — ~7,982 SF, furnished, 15 offices, 2 conference rooms, reception area
> - **Suite 240** — ~2,814 SF, open concept with natural sunlight, 2 offices, conference room
> - Both suites can be combined for ~10,800 SF
>
> It's a full-service lease — your rent covers utilities, janitorial, 24/7 security, and building access.
>
> If you'd like to learn more or schedule a tour, I'd love to connect you with Nick directly.
>
> Best regards,
> [VA Name]
> WePartner Commercial
> On behalf of Nick Kavali
> [Verizon OneTalk number]

### Day 3 — Phone Call (Attempt 2)

- **Channel:** Verizon OneTalk
- **Action:** Second call attempt
- **If voicemail:** Leave Voicemail 2 (shorter, reference first call and email)
- **If no answer:** Log. Move to Day 12.

### Day 12 — Final Follow-Up Email

- **Channel:** Outlook
- **Action:** Short, low-pressure follow-up
- **Template:**

> **Subject:** Re: Office Space Opportunity in Roswell — [Business Name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Just circling back on my note from last week. No pressure at all — if the timing isn't right, I completely understand.
>
> If your office needs change down the road, we'd love to be a resource. Suites are going fast at this location.
>
> Feel free to reach out anytime.
>
> Best,
> [VA Name]
> WePartner Commercial

### After the Initial Sequence — Classify & Route

| Outcome | Classification | Next Step |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| Engaged positively at any point | HOT or WARM | Follow respective cadence below |
| No response after all 3 attempts | COOL | Wait 1 month, then one more call |
| Said "not interested" with no reason to re-engage | COLD | Log reason. Close out. |
| Shared lease expiry or said "check back at [date]" | FUTURE | Set calendar reminder |
| Clearly not a fit (too small, wrong area, long lease) | DISQUALIFIED | Log reason. Remove from active list. |

## HOT Lead Cadence

**These leads want to act NOW. Your job is to hand off to Nick and keep things warm until he connects.**

| When | Action |
|------|--------|
| **Within 5 minutes of call** | Email Nick (CC Shelly) with full HOT lead summary |
| **Within 2 hours** | Send follow-up email to the lead confirming you'll have Nick reach out |
| **Next day** | Check with Nick: "Were you able to connect with [Lead]?" |
| **3 days later** | If Nick hasn't connected yet, check in with the lead: "Wanted to make sure you and Nick got a chance to connect." |
| **1 week later** | If still no connection, flag to Nick again. This lead is cooling. |
| **2 weeks** | If no progress, reclassify as WARM |

---

## WARM Lead Cadence

**These leads showed interest but aren't acting yet. Keep them engaged without being pushy.**

| Timing | Channel | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Immediately after call** | Email | Send the follow-up email with property details |
| **1 week later** | Phone | Quick check-in: "Following up on our conversation — any questions?" |
| **2 weeks later** | Email | Value-add touch: share a property highlight, neighborhood feature, or new tenant |
| **3 weeks later** | Phone | Brief check-in: "Just wanted to see if anything has changed on your end." |
| **4 weeks later** | Email | Gentle last touch: "Still here if you need us." |
| **After 4 weeks with no movement** | — | **Transition to Monthly** |

---

## Monthly Re-Engagement (Cool / No Response)

Nick's preference: **1 month between re-engagement attempts.** This is a conservative cadence — respect it.

| Timing | Channel | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **1 month after last contact** | Phone | Brief, direct re-engagement call: "Hi [Name], this is [VA Name] from WePartner Commercial. I reached out to you last month about the office space at Mansell Office Park. Just checking in to see if anything has changed on your end." |
| **Immediately after call** | Email or SMS | Short follow-up: "Thanks for your time — just wanted to keep you in the loop. Suites are still available." |

### After Monthly Re-Engagement:

| Outcome | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **They engage** | Reclassify as WARM or HOT. Follow that cadence. |
| **Still no interest (WARM lead)** | Try one SMS 1 month later. If no response after that, drop. |
| **Still no interest (COOL/COLD lead)** | Drop from active cadence. Mark as closed. |

---

## SMS Guidelines

SMS is used **sparingly** — only for warm leads after the monthly re-engagement attempt shows no progress.

**SMS Template:**

> Hi [Name], this is [VA Name] from WePartner Commercial. We still have move-in ready office suites available at Mansell Office Park in Roswell. If your space needs change, we'd love to be a resource. Feel free to reach out anytime! — [VA Name], WePartner Commercial

**Rules:**
- Only 1 SMS per lead, ever (unless they respond)
- Only used after a call + email cycle has been completed
- Never the first point of contact
- If they respond, continue the conversation via their preferred channel

## FUTURE Lead Cadence (Lease Expiry / Timeline Known)

**These are your highest-value long-term leads. They WILL need space — just not yet.**

| Timing | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **12 months before expiry** | Email: "I know your lease is coming up next year. Wanted to plant the seed early — we have great options in Roswell." |
| **6 months before expiry** | Phone: "Your lease is up in about 6 months. Would it be worth taking a look at your options before renewal time?" |
| **3 months before expiry** | Phone + Email: "We'd love to have you tour Mansell Office Park before you make your renewal decision." |
| **1 month before expiry** | Final outreach: "Just wanted to make sure you've had a chance to explore your options. Nick would love to show you the space." |

## Quarterly Touch (Minimal — Leads with Small Motivation)

For leads who showed even a tiny spark of interest but aren't in any other cadence:

| Quarter | Channel | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Q1 | Email | Market update: "Quick update on Mansell Office Park..." |
| Q2 | Phone | Brief check-in |
| Q3 | Email | "As you plan for next year, keep us in mind..." |
| Q4 | Email | Year-end touch |

**After 1 year with no response:** Review with Nick. Remove from list or keep on annual touch.

---

## Channel Rules

- **After every call (successful or voicemail):** Send a follow-up email within 2 hours on the same day
- Never call more than 2x in a row without sending an email
- Never email more than 2x in a row without calling
- SMS only after call + email cycle with no response (warm leads only)
- Alternate channels to avoid feeling repetitive

## When to STOP Following Up

**Stop and close out the lead if:**
- They explicitly said "Do not contact me again"
- They just signed a long-term lease (5+ years) with no indication they'd consider moving
- The business is clearly too small or too large for the available suites
- The business has no office space needs (e.g., purely field-based)
- You've completed the full cadence cycle (initial + 1 month re-engagement + SMS for warm) with zero engagement
- Nick tells you to stop

**When in doubt:** Ask Nick. Include it in your daily report: "Should I continue following up with [Business]? Here's what happened..."
