Who Does What — AR Board

Clear ownership, no confusion

Fiona (Day-to-Day)

  • Maintains the board
  • Sends follow-up emails/calls to clients
  • Moves cards through the pipeline
  • Logs notes and activity on each card
  • Sets due dates for next follow-up
  • Flags items that need escalation

Brandon (Support)

  • Provides invoices when requested
  • Provides client contact info
  • Confirms payments received & applied in QB

Brandon's simple rule: If you see cards in the "Needs Invoice/Info" column, that's Fiona waiting on you. Drop the info on the card and she takes it from there.
