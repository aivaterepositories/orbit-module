## Engagement Summary

**This is a PROCESS ONLY engagement — no VA sourcing or placement.**

The scope is building a complete LinkedIn Outreach / Investor Lead Generation system for Jeremy to use as a scalable outbound channel. The LinkedIn program is positioned as both a short-term lead gen tool AND a long-term relationship/network asset for future capital raises and product offers.

### What We're Building
A repeatable, documented LinkedIn outreach system that:
- Gets Jeremy in front of highly qualified investor and advisor prospects
- Books meetings directly into his calendar
- Builds a growing LinkedIn network asset for future use
- Replaces spray-and-pray marketing with targeted, ICP-driven outreach

## Campaign Concepts (from Discovery Call)

### Targeting Concepts
- Start with known investor database, target decision-makers via LinkedIn
- Real estate investors currently lending capital at modest returns
- Financial advisors serving clients with underperforming real estate holdings
- Geo-targeted campaigns into affluent areas or markets with investor density
- Competitive campaigns against other turnkey operators

### Value Proposition Themes
- Former financial advisor who saw widespread underperforming real estate on client balance sheets
- Bedrock built to solve that without requiring investors to give up ownership and control
- Single-family residential as strong risk-adjusted asset class when bought right and managed properly
- Differentiated from syndications/passive vehicles — clients retain direct ownership and upside

### Funnel Concepts
- LinkedIn profile repositioned around Bedrock offering
- Outbound connection requests + targeted messaging to ICPs
- Conversation management → book into Jeremy's calendar
- CEO roundtable / webinar as higher-conviction conversion asset
- Growing LinkedIn network as long-term asset for future capital raises
