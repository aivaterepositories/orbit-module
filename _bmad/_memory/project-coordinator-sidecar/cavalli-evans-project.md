## Follow-Up Cadence (Updated — March 26 PEC)

- **After every call (successful or voicemail):** Send follow-up email or SMS within a couple hours same day
- **Hot/warm leads:** Same-day email handoff to Nick (CC Shelly)
- **Re-engagement (no response):** Wait 1 month, then re-engage via phone call (brief, straightforward)
- **Warm leads after 1 month with no progress:** Try SMS one more month later, then drop
- **Super cold leads:** Drop after initial sequence + 1 month re-engagement attempt
- **Future leads (shared lease expiry):** Track timeline, re-engage 3-6 months before expiry
- **Quarterly:** Light touch for leads who showed any small motivation
- **Key principle:** Office leasing is unique — prospects may have 5-year leases. Don't waste VA time on clearly mismatched leads. Note why they said no and move on.
- **Nick's preference:** Conservative. Don't bombard. 1 month between re-engagement attempts is the standard.

## Handoff Process (Updated — March 26 PEC)

- **Primary handoff method: EMAIL** — Nick does not carry the OneTalk number on his cell, so live transfers are not standard practice
- **Hot lead:** VA sends immediate email summary to Nick (CC Shelly). Nick contacts lead directly. Include: business name, contact name, phone, email, 2-3 sentence summary of conversation, availability/timeline mentioned
- **VA cannot answer a question:** Tell lead "I can get that answered for you shortly" → consult Nick → follow up
- **VA does NOT schedule tours** — Nick manages his own calendar and tour scheduling
- **Shelly CC'd on ALL communications** (at least during initial ramp period)
- **Warm lead / good conversation:** Include in daily lead report email
- **Daily report:** End of shift, VA sends lead sheet (phone, email, business name, contact, call notes)
- **Nick may ask VA follow-up questions** before calling leads back
- **Future possibility:** After 3-4 months, if VA improves, may graduate to sitting in on Nick's calls (three-way) to learn
