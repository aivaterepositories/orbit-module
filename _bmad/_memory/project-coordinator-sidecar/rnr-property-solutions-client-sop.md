## 1. Rental Market Analysis

**Purpose:** Ensure competitive rental pricing and attract quality tenants.
**Tools:** Zillow Rentals, TurboTenant, MLS, FB Marketplace

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Gather Market Data:**
   - Search for similar rental listings on Zillow Rentals, TurboTenant, and Craigslist.
   - Check Rentometer for rental rate comparisons.
   - Review local property management websites.
   - Analyze current rental listings on MLS (if applicable) and FB Marketplace.

2. **Compare and Adjust:**
   - Compare properties by size, condition, location, and amenities.
   - Adjust rent based on features (garage, pool, updated kitchen, etc.).
   - Set a competitive rent price to maximize occupancy while staying profitable.

3. **Approval and Implementation:**
   - Finalize rental price and update the listing accordingly.
   - Adjust price based on market demand and interest level.

4. **Final rent amount approved by Willie.**

## 2. Listing and Marketing Rental Properties

**Purpose:** Maximize property exposure to attract qualified tenants.
**Tools:** TurboTenant, Zillow Rentals, Facebook Marketplace, Furnished Finders

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Create Listing on TurboTenant:**
   - Log in to TurboTenant and add a new listing.
   - Input property details, rent amount, deposit, and lease terms.
   - Upload high-quality photos and a detailed description.
   - Ensure compliance with Fair Housing Laws.

2. **Post Listing to Zillow Rentals:**
   - Sync TurboTenant with Zillow Rentals (or manually post if needed).
   - Monitor listing performance and update if necessary.

3. **Market on Additional Platforms:**
   - Post on Facebook Marketplace and relevant local groups.
   - Furnished Finders primarily for rent-by-room listing.

4. **Monitor and Respond to Inquiries:**
   - Set up automated responses through TurboTenant.
   - Respond to messages within 24 hours.
   - Direct inquiries from all marketing channels to TurboTenant pre-screener.
   - If they call or text, copy paste: *"Thank you for your interest. Before we can show home, we request you fill out the pre-screener to see if our home would be a good fit."*
   - Schedule showings only if filled out pre-screener.

## 3. Tenant Screening Process

**Purpose:** Select responsible tenants who will pay on time and maintain the property.
**Tools:** TurboTenant Screening, Zillow's Background Check

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Pre-Screening Questions:**
   - Ask about move-in date, employment, income, and rental history.
   - Verify they meet the income requirement (typically 3x rent).
   - Confirm no prior evictions or felony convictions.

2. **Application Process:**
   - Direct applicants to complete the online application via TurboTenant.
   - Require a $45 application fee to cover background checks.

3. **Background and Credit Check:**
   - Review credit score (preferably 620+ but flexible based on income).
   - Verify employment and income with pay stubs or tax returns.
   - Check rental history, including past evictions and references from landlords.
   - Check IDIcore for background check.

4. **Approval and Lease Agreement:**
   - Approve qualified tenants and notify them via email.
   - Prepare and send lease agreement using TurboTenant's e-signature tool.
   - Collect security deposit and first month's rent before move-in.
   - Follow rent instruction email for payments.

## 4. Move-In Process

**Purpose:** Ensure smooth transition and proper documentation.
**Tools:** TurboTenant Lease, Google Drive (for document storage), Property Inspection App

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Schedule Move-In Inspection:**
   - Conduct a walkthrough with the tenant using a checklist.
   - Take photos and videos as evidence of the property's condition.

2. **Provide Tenant Welcome Packet:**
   - Give physical packet — leave at house.

3. **Confirm Key Handover:**
   - Handover keys and access codes (if applicable).
   - Ensure tenant signs a receipt confirming key acceptance.

---

## 5. Rent Collection and Late Payment Handling

**Purpose:** Ensure consistent rent payments and address late payments.
**Tools:** TurboTenant Payment Processing, Zelle, Venmo, or Bank Transfers

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Set Up Automatic Payments:**
   - Encourage tenants to set up auto pay with their bank.
   - Email rent payment instructions.

2. **Monitor Rent Payments:**
   - Check TurboTenant for payment status.
   - Send reminders 5 days before rent is due.

3. **Handle Late Payments:**
   - Send automated late payment notices through TurboTenant.
   - Charge late fees as per the lease agreement.
   - If unpaid after 7 days, issue a "3-Day Pay or Quit" notice.

4. **Payment Collection:**
   - Monitor Wells Fargo banking account.
   - Match incoming Zelle, ACH, deposit with charge amount in TurboTenant.
   - Record payment in TurboTenant (will send tenant receipt).

## 6. Maintenance and Repair Management

**Purpose:** Keep properties in good condition and comply with habitability laws.
**Tools:** TurboTenant Maintenance Requests, Google Sheets (for tracking requests)

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Receive and Track Maintenance Requests:**
   - Tenants submit requests through TurboTenant.
   - Assign priority level (Emergency, Urgent, Routine).

2. **Dispatch Repairs:**
   - Contact preferred contractors or handyman services.
   - **Osbel is main POC for all maintenance requests. Send WhatsApp message.**
   - Approve estimates and schedule repairs within 24-48 hours.

3. **Follow-Up with Tenant:**
   - Confirm repair completion and tenant satisfaction.
   - Keep records of all repairs and invoices.
   - Mark as resolved or dismissed in TurboTenant under maintenance section.

---

## 7. Lease Renewal or Move-Out Process

**Purpose:** Retain tenants or prepare property for re-rental.
**Tools:** TurboTenant Lease Renewal, Google Docs (for notices)

### Lease Renewal Process

1. **Review Market Rent (60 Days Before Lease Expiry):**
   - Conduct rental market analysis to determine renewal rates.
   - Notify tenant of renewal terms via email.

2. **Send Lease Renewal Offer (45 Days Before Lease Expiry):**
   - Use TurboTenant to generate a renewal lease.
   - Require tenant signature within 10 days.

3. **Confirm Renewal or Prepare for Move-Out:**
   - If renewing, update lease terms and collect new security deposit (if applicable).
   - If moving out, schedule a pre-move-out inspection.

### Move-Out Process

1. Tenant must provide at least **60 days' notice**.

2. **Conduct Final Inspection:**
   - Compare move-out condition to move-in checklist.
   - Deduct any damages from security deposit as per lease agreement.

3. **Refund Security Deposit (within 30 Days):**
   - Calculate deductions and provide an itemized list.
   - Process refund via TurboTenant, bank transfer, Zelle, or mail check.

## 8. Communication

**Purpose:** Ensure all tenant inquiries or requests are handled timely and vendors are sent out for work as needed.
**Tools:** CRM, WhatsApp, Email

All communication needs to be documented in CRM, WhatsApp, or email:
- **WhatsApp:** Used for Spanish-speaking tenants and contractors/vendors
- **CRM:** Used to call and text with tenants and also any vendors or contractors
- **Email:** Used primarily to send payment instructions or documents needing to be sent

## 9. Utilities

**Purpose:** Ensure all utilities are turned on and off when needed between tenants and maintenance.
**Tools:** Local company services

### Utility Providers
- **City of Fort Walton Beach** — water and trash
- **Okaloosa County** — water and trash
- **FPL** — electricity
- **Chelco** — electricity
- **City of Crestview** — water and trash
- **Okaloosa Gas** — gas
- **Auburn Water** — water
- **Waste Management** — trash

### Responsibilities
1. PM responsibility to ensure all utilities are started.
2. PM must also cancel off all utilities once new tenant moves in.
3. PM will ensure all utilities for rentals that are active are paid and current.
