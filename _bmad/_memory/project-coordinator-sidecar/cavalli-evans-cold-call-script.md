## Important Reminders Before Every Call

- You are **NOT a broker.** You are calling on behalf of Nick Kavali / WePartner Commercial as a leasing support team member.
- You do **NOT negotiate deal terms** — no pricing discussions, no lease negotiations.
- You do **NOT schedule tours** — capture the lead's availability and hand off to Nick.
- Your goal: **Generate interest → Capture contact info → Get permission to send details or connect with Nick → Hand off via email.**
- Speak with confidence. You're offering a premium opportunity to a select group of businesses, not asking for a favor.
- Smile when you talk — it comes through in your voice.
- **This is a living document.** It will be revised as you learn what works and what doesn't. Expect multiple versions over time.

## SCRIPT 1: Gatekeeper / Front Desk

### Opening

> "Good morning! I'm looking to speak to the person who handles leasing or office space decisions for **[business name]**."

### If asked: "What is this in regards to?" / "Is there something I can help you with?"

> "Sure, thank you. My name is **[VA Name]** with WePartner Commercial. I was looking at your website and I think we have a leasing opportunity at a professional office property in Roswell that might be of interest to **[business name]**."

### If they say: "Nobody is available right now."

> "Oh, certainly. I completely understand — this may not be the best time. Before I go, may I ask who would be the right person for me to follow up with? Do you happen to know if they have a direct line or email address where I could reach them?"

**Always capture:** Name, title, direct phone, and/or email. Log immediately in the tracker.

### If they say: "Let me transfer you to [Name]."

> "Perfect — thank you so much!"

**Transition to Script 2 (Principal Contact) — adjust your opening to match the transfer context.**

### If they ask: "Can you send an email instead?"

> "Absolutely! I'd be happy to send over some details. What would be the best email address for me to send it to? And who should I address it to?"

**Capture the email, send the follow-up email template (see Follow-Up Cadence doc), and log in tracker.**

## SCRIPT 2: Principal Contact / Decision Maker

Nick designed this script to be informational rather than question-heavy — he intentionally avoids questions that force the VA into multiple branching paths. The concept: give them the opportunity, see if they bite, and route to Nick.

**Below are MULTIPLE VERSIONS of the intro. Use the version that best fits how the conversation is going.**

---

### VERSION A: Standard Introduction (Default — Use This First)

**Best for:** Cold call to a direct contact from the Seamless.ai or existing list. They don't know you're calling.

> "Good morning, **[Name]**! My name is **[VA Name]** and I'm calling from WePartner Commercial. I want to let you know about a really great leasing opportunity that's come up at a professional office building here in Roswell.
>
> If there's any chance you're looking to relocate or expand **[business name]** into the booming Roswell and Alpharetta markets, I thought you might be particularly interested in this property.
>
> Are you familiar with the intersection of Mansell Road and Houze Road, by any chance?"

**If YES (they know the area):**
> "Perfect — so you know the area well. This is Mansell Office Park, right at that signaled corner. Less than two miles from GA-400, surrounded by great dining and shopping. Our management team asked me to reach out to a select group of businesses to share this opportunity."

**If NO (they don't know the area):**
> "No problem at all! It's a prime location in Roswell — right off GA-400, surrounded by restaurants, retail, and walking trails. Very accessible for both employees and clients. Our management team asked me to share this with a select group of businesses we think would be a great fit."

**Then close with:**
> "We'll be coordinating tours over the next month. If you're interested in visiting the property, I'd love to introduce you to Nick Kavali — he can walk you through the spaces and answer any questions. Or if you're too busy right now, I can send you an email with some details. May I ask for a good email address?"

---

### VERSION B: The Quick Pitch (For Busy / Rushed Contacts)

**Best for:** When you can tell they're busy, distracted, or about to hang up. Get to the point in 15 seconds.

> "Hi **[Name]**, I'll be brief — this is **[VA Name]** from WePartner Commercial. We have professional office suites available in Roswell, move-in ready, full-service lease. Sizes range from about 2,800 to 8,000 square feet. Can I send you a quick email with the details? It'll take 30 seconds to skim."

**If yes:** Capture email. Send template. Log.
**If no:** "No problem at all — appreciate your time!" Log and move on.

---

### VERSION C: The Value-Lead (For When You Have Intel About the Business)

**Best for:** When you've done research — you know their industry, size, or current situation from Crexie/Google/LinkedIn.

> "Good morning, **[Name]**! My name is **[VA Name]** from WePartner Commercial. I noticed **[business name]** is a **[industry — e.g., growing law firm / insurance agency / consulting practice]** in the area, and I thought you might be interested in something we have available.
>
> We manage Mansell Office Park in Roswell — it's a professional office building with a great mix of tenants, including law firms, insurance, medical, and tech companies. We have move-in ready suites with a full-service lease that covers utilities, janitorial, security — the works.
>
> Would it be worth sending you a quick overview?"

---

### VERSION D: The Referral Intro

**Best for:** When someone on a previous call said "You should talk to [Name]" or "Try calling [Company]."

> "Good morning, **[Name]**! My name is **[VA Name]** from WePartner Commercial. I was actually speaking with **[Referrer Name]** recently, and they suggested I reach out to you. We have professional office space available in Roswell that might be a good fit for **[business name]**. Would you have a minute to hear about it?"

---

### VERSION E: The Warm Follow-Up Call (After Email Was Sent)

**Best for:** Calling someone you previously emailed.

> "Hi **[Name]**, this is **[VA Name]** from WePartner Commercial. I sent you an email earlier this week about office space at Mansell Office Park in Roswell — just wanted to make sure it came through and see if you had any questions."

**If they read it:** "Great! What did you think? Would you be interested in learning more?"
**If they haven't:** "No problem — the quick version is: move-in ready suites, full-service lease, prime Roswell location right off 400. Would it be worth a 30-second overview?"

---

### VERSION F: The Re-Engagement Call (1 Month Later)

**Best for:** Re-contacting someone from the monthly cadence who didn't respond initially.

> "Hi **[Name]**, this is **[VA Name]** from WePartner Commercial. I reached out to you about a month ago regarding the office space at Mansell Office Park in Roswell. Just checking in to see if anything has changed on your end — any office space needs on the horizon?"

**Keep it brief.** If they're still not interested, thank them and move on.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

These are questions prospects commonly ask. Know the answers cold.

### Q: "How much is the rent?"

> "Great question — the spaces are competitively priced and offered as a full-service lease, which means your base rent covers utilities, janitorial, security, and 24/7 access. For the specific numbers, Nick Kavali would be the best person to walk you through that based on your needs. Would you like me to have him reach out?"

**NEVER quote $/RSF. Always route to Nick.**

### Q: "What does 'full-service lease' mean?"

> "A full-service lease means that your base rent payment includes utilities, nighttime janitorial services, daytime Day Porter services, 24/7 building surveillance, and 24/7 access controls for your staff. It simplifies your overhead significantly — one payment covers the big operational items."

### Q: "What's an Opportunity Zone? Why should I care?"

> "The property is located within a federally designated Opportunity Zone, which was created by the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act. It's designed to incentivize long-term investment in the area through preferential tax treatment. The specifics depend on your situation, so Nick or your accountant would be the best people to walk through the details."

**Keep it high-level. Don't try to explain tax law.**

### Q: "What sizes are available?"

> "We currently have two suites on the second floor of the 695 Mansell Road building. One is about 7,982 square feet — it's fully furnished with 15 offices, 2 conference rooms, and a large open area. The other is about 2,814 square feet with an open concept layout and amazing natural light. They can also be combined for about 10,800 square feet total."

### Q: "Is the space furnished?"

> "The larger suite — Suite 200 — is fully furnished and move-in ready. The smaller suite — Suite 240 — is not furnished but has a great open layout. Both are ready for immediate occupancy."

### Q: "What floor are the suites on?"

> "Both suites are on the second floor of the 695 Mansell Road building."

### Q: "Is there parking?"

> "Yes — ample free parking on-site."

### Q: "Who else is in the building?"

> "The building has a great professional mix — Keller Williams, a coworking space, mortgage services, medical and health companies, a law firm, information management, and insurance. It's a diverse, professional environment."

### Q: "Can we get signage?"

> "Yes — monument signage is available. The property sits on a highly visible signaled corner at Mansell and Houze Roads. Nick can walk you through the signage options."

### Q: "When can we move in?"

> "The suites are move-in ready. For specific timing and coordination, Nick would be the best person to work that out with you. Would you like me to have him reach out?"

### Q: "Can the space be modified / built out?"

> "That's a great question — Nick would be the best person to discuss any modifications or build-out options. I can have him reach out to you to talk through what's possible."

### Q: "I have a question you didn't cover here."

> "That's a great question — I want to make sure I give you the most accurate answer. Let me check with Nick and get back to you shortly. What's the best way to reach you?"

**Then email Nick the question, get the answer, and follow up with the lead.**

## Common Objections & Best Responses

*(Full details in the Objection Handling Guide. This is a quick-reference.)*

| Objection | Quick Response |
|-----------|---------------|
| "Not interested" | "Completely understand. Can I send you a quick email for your files in case things change?" |
| "We're happy where we are" | "Glad to hear it. When does your current lease come up? A lot of businesses like to explore options 6-12 months out." |
| "Just signed a lease" | "Congratulations! May I ask how long the term is? I like to keep track for when the time comes." |
| "Too busy right now" | "Totally respect your time. Can I send you a 30-second email with the highlights?" |
| "Send me an email" | "Absolutely! What's the best address? And are you looking in the 2,800 to 8,000 SF range?" |
| "How much?" | "It's competitively priced, full-service. Nick can walk you through the numbers. Want me to connect you?" |
| "Need to check with partner" | "Makes sense. I'll send details you can share. When's a good time to follow up?" |
| "We work remotely" | "A lot of companies find a hybrid setup helps for client meetings and team culture. Can I send info for your files?" |
| "Roswell is too far" | "Understand. It's <2 miles from GA-400 off Mansell Rd exit. Know anyone who might be looking in this area?" |
| "We're downsizing" | "We have a smaller suite — ~2,800 SF, full-service, could simplify overhead. Worth a quick email?" |
| "Never heard of WePartner" | "We manage Mansell Office Park in Roswell — Keller Williams, a law firm, medical and insurance tenants. Nick Kavali is the Property Manager." |
| "I don't have an answer for that" (you're stuck) | "Great question — let me check with Nick and get back to you. What's the best way to reach you?" |

## Quick Reference — What You CAN and CANNOT Say

| CAN Say | CANNOT Say |
|---------|------------|
| "We have office space available" | "The rent is $19.50 per square foot" |
| "It's a full-service lease" | "I can negotiate a lower rate for you" |
| "Nick can walk you through the pricing" | "We can probably do $X for you" |
| "The suites range from ~2,800 to ~8,000 sq ft" | Any specific lease terms or conditions |
| "I'm calling from Nick Kavali's office" | "I'm a broker with WePartner" |
| "I'll have Nick reach out to you" | "Let me schedule you for Thursday" |
| "Let me check with Nick and get back to you" | Making up an answer you're not sure about |
| "The building is in an Opportunity Zone" | Specific tax advice or financial details |
| "General office use is what we accommodate" | "We don't accept [type of business]" |
