## What "Full Service Lease" Means

**This is a MAJOR selling point. Know it cold.**

A full-service lease means the tenant's base rent payment includes:

- Utilities (electric, water, HVAC)
- Nighttime janitorial services
- Daytime Day Porter services
- 24/7 building surveillance
- 24/7 access controls for staff

**Why it matters to the prospect:** One predictable monthly payment. No surprise utility bills. No separate contracts for cleaning, security, or building maintenance. It simplifies their overhead.

**How to position it:**
> "It's a full-service lease, so your rent covers everything — utilities, cleaning, security, and 24/7 access. One payment, no surprises."

## Opportunity Zone — What It Means

Mansell Office Park is located within a federally designated **Opportunity Zone (OZ)**.

**What is an OZ?** A community where businesses and investors can receive preferential tax treatment. Created by the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act to incentivize long-term private investment in real estate, infrastructure, and business development.

**Who cares about this?**
- Business owners who are also investors
- Companies looking at long-term real estate commitments
- Financial advisors and accountants advising on tax strategy

**How to mention it (only if relevant):**
> "The property is also located within an Opportunity Zone, which can offer some tax advantages depending on your situation. Nick or your accountant would be the best people to walk through the specifics."

**Do NOT try to explain the tax details yourself.** Just mention it exists and route to Nick.
